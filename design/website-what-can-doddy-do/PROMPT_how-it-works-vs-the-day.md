# Prompt: does "How it works" agree with "What can Doddy do?"

Copy everything under the line into a fresh Claude Code session on `Bienos/doddy.2`. It is read-only until step 6. The "Known findings" at the end come from a read of the copy on 14 October 2026; they are leads to confirm, not verdicts.

---

You are auditing the Doddy website (`site/`, live at https://www.doddy.lol) for three problems between its two ways of explaining the product:

- **A. How it works**: `site/index.html`, sections `#how` (three steps), `.receipt-section`, `.voice`, `.privacy`, `.later`, and the hero/film/fine print around them.
- **B. What Doddy can do**: `site/what-can-doddy-do/index.html`, generated by `tools/build_day.py`, words in `design/website-what-can-doddy-do/COPY.md`, plus the homepage teaser `.moments-teaser`.

The three problems:

1. **Repetition.** The same idea, claim or phrase said more than once on a page, or said on the homepage and again on page B without adding anything.
2. **Contradiction.** Two places that cannot both be true: a different scope, a different status (built / in testing / later), a different data claim, a different count.
3. **Sense.** A visitor who reads A then B (or B then A) should end up with one picture of Doddy. Say where the story breaks: a jump in scope, a promise one page makes that the other doesn't keep, a section whose job isn't clear.

## Rules (from CLAUDE.md, the constitution and the site README)

- Read `STATUS.md`, `site/README.md` ("What's real on the page", "What can Doddy do?"), `design/website-what-can-doddy-do/COPY.md` and `docs/implementation/V1_VOICE_STATUS.md` first. Don't read `DECISIONS.md` whole; search it for ADR 468 to 471.
- **Only claim what the repo can prove.** Check every capability in A and B against `docs/implementation/` status docs and the app code under `DoddyApp/` and `Packages/DoddyCore/`. Label each claim `proved (file:line)`, `not provable from the repo`, or `contradicted (file:line)`. Never mark anything as working because the page says so.
- The three status words are fixed: **Built** (works in the app now), **In testing** (built, being tried on real iPhones), **Later, maybe** (not built, no dates). Don't invent a fourth.
- Character art comes only from the owner's pack (`site/assets/day/`). No new Doddy drawings.
- Doddy is an AI: no claimed feelings, no guilt, no jokes in sensitive moments. Data claims must match the privacy panel and the app's "What leaves this iPhone" list.
- **Steps 1 to 5 change nothing.** No edits under `site/`, no deploy, no push to the default branch.

## Steps

1. **Inventory.** Build one table of every claim on A and on B: where it is (file, section, line), the exact words, the status it implies, the data it implies leaves the phone (none / voice vendor / server / iCloud), and who says it (page or Doddy's bubble).
2. **Repetition pass.** Group claims that say the same thing. For each group, list every place, then decide: *keep once* (where, why), *merge*, or *keep both because the second adds something* (say what). Count phrases that recur ("Your words, not a guess", "Doddy is an AI, not a person", "Later, maybe", "stored on your iPhone"). Intentional echoes (a sign-off, a legal line) are fine; say so.
3. **Contradiction pass.** Compare A to B on: scope (notes only vs a ten-thing assistant), setup ("no account, no setup" vs switching things on in Settings), data ("all on your iPhone" vs weather, Wikipedia, maps, shared lists), status (a scene labelled Built that depends on something In testing), and what each page lists under "Later, maybe". For every pair, quote both sides and say which one the repo supports.
4. **Sense pass.** Read A top to bottom as a first-time visitor, then B, then both in the order a visitor would click. Write the story each page tells in one sentence. If the sentences disagree, say where. List every section and give it one job; flag sections with no job or two.
5. **Report.** A table: `#`, type (repeat / contradiction / sense), place A, place B, evidence, severity (blocks trust / confusing / cosmetic), recommended fix, who decides (owner copy call / engineering fact check). Then a recommended page order for the homepage with each section's single job.
6. **Only if I say "apply it":** change the smallest set of words and sections, regenerate with `python3 -I tools/build_day.py` where the copy deck is involved, update `COPY.md`, and run `cd site && npm test`, `python3 -I tools/build_day.py --check`, `python3 -I tools/build_blog.py --check`. Report the commands and their output. Don't say a check passed that you didn't run.

## Output rules

Plain words, short sentences, no filler. Quote the site's exact text when pointing at it. Keep proposed new copy separate from existing copy and mark it "proposed". If something can't be decided from the repo, put it under "Owner decisions" with the options.

## Known findings from the first read (confirm each in step 3)

**Repetition**
- R1. Saving and finding a note is told four times: the hero chip (spare keys), `#how` steps 1 and 2, the receipt section, and the 18:45 "My brain has left the chat" scene in the homepage teaser (and again as moment 7 on page B).
- R2. "Your words, not a guess / not mine" appears in step 2 (`says-small`), the receipt section's claim, and the 18:45 scene.
- R3. "Doddy is an AI, not a person" is in the hero, the privacy panel, the footer and page B's fine print. Probably fine in the footer and hero; the rest is worth a look.
- R4. "Later, maybe" is a separate section on A (Android only) and a separate section on B (translation, restaurant picking, Android). Two lists of "not built" that don't match.

**Contradictions**
- C1. `#how` says "Three steps, all on your iPhone. No account, no setup." Page B's scenes need things switched on (calendar in Settings, shared lists in iCloud) and send data off the phone (weather via Open-Meteo, Wikipedia lookups, both asked for by ElevenLabs' servers). Does "all on your iPhone" survive that? The privacy note already lists what leaves the phone; `#how` doesn't point at it.
- C2. The 18:45 scene is labelled **Built** ("Works today"), but it is a spoken question, and voice is **In testing** on the homepage (`.voice` pill). Typed search is Built; voice recall isn't. Which one does the scene show?
- C3. The homepage voice section says Doddy "looks through your notes and answers from what you saved". Page B shows voice doing calendar, weather, maps, lists, reminders and plans. Same feature, two different scopes.
- C4. The homepage privacy note says "Everything else stays on your iPhone", while page B's captions name outside services for weather and Wikipedia. Check that the voice-conversation line is meant to cover them, and say so if it is.
- C5. The film chapters "Every language at once" and "Asking a local" sit above page B's "Lost in translation. Not built yet." The film caption says "A story about Doddy, not a demo", which may be enough; confirm.

**Sense**
- S1. The homepage tells a notes story (A little less to carry), page B tells an assistant story (a mate who handles ten small problems). Nothing on the homepage says how one becomes the other, or that only notes are Built today.
- S2. The teaser picks one Built scene and two In-testing scenes but doesn't say so in its heading, so a visitor can read all three as shipped.
- S3. The `.receipt-section` and `#how` step 2 do the same job. One of them can lose its place or be folded into the other.
- S4. The voice section comes after the teaser, so voice is introduced as new even though two of the three teaser scenes are spoken.
