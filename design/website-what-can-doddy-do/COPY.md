# "What can Doddy do?" — the copy deck

The words on the page, one place to edit them. Mockup 14 is this file rendered. Handwriting lines are Doddy's voice (Caveat); "You say" lines are the visitor's; cards are what the app would show. Revised after the owner's review of 13 October (moments 2 to 10).

## Hero

- Pill: One ordinary day, ten small problems
- Headline: **What can Doddy do?** *Quite a lot, actually.*
- Supporting line: Ten random moments from an ordinary day, and what a mate in your pocket does with them.
- Buttons: Start the day · Join early access
- Fine print: Scripted demonstrations, each labelled with what's built today. Doddy is an AI, not a person.
- Doddy in the hero: *Pick one.*

## The moments

### 08:45 · You're late. Again. — Calendar and messages · in testing
- Problem: The alarm went off. You went back to sleep. The meeting didn't.
- You say: Doddy, what's happening today?
- Doddy says: *Well, your meeting started 15 minutes ago. Excellent start.*
- Doddy does: Reads today's calendar out loud, with the bit you'd rather not hear first. · Drafts a two-line "running late" message. You tap send, Doddy never does. · Moves the next thing back twenty minutes, after you tap Add.
- Card: Today — Team meeting 09:00, started · Dentist 12:30 · Call Mum 19:00
- Caption: Scripted demonstration. The calendar is read only after you switch it on in Settings.
- Then (Doddy walks): *Right. Coffee first.*

### 09:10 · Jacket or no jacket? — Weather · in testing
- Problem: The window says sunny. The window has lied before.
- You say: Jacket or no jacket?
- Doddy says: *17 degrees now, rain at four. Jacket. Obviously.*
- Doddy does: Checks the forecast for where you are, when you ask. · Says the useful bit first. The small talk is optional. · Tells you what left the phone: the place name, nothing else.
- Card: Warsaw, now — 17°, cloudy 09:10 · Rain from 16:00
- Caption: Scripted demonstration. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, asked for by ElevenLabs' servers.

### 10:20 · The awkward text. — Message drafts · in testing
- Problem: Three drafts. Two deleted. One "hey" sent by accident.
- Alex wrote (shown in the scene and on the draft card): Yesterday was fun :)
- You say: Help me reply to this without sounding desperate.
- Doddy says: *That's a fairly ambitious request, but okay.*
- Doddy does: Writes a draft in your voice, not his. · Three nudges: funnier, shorter, more confident. · Opens Messages with the draft. Sending stays your job.
- Card: Draft to Alex — replying to "Yesterday was fun :)" (Alex, 10:04). Funnier: "Yesterday was fun. Same again next week? No pressure, my calendar is mostly staring at walls." · Shorter: "Yesterday was fun. Next week?" · More confident: "Yesterday was fun. Let's do Thursday, same place. I'll book."
- Caption: Scripted demonstration. Doddy never sends anything.
- Aside: *I don't send. You send.*

### 13:00 · What should we eat? — Maps · in testing
- Problem: Two hungry people. Forty minutes of "I don't mind."
- You say: Find somewhere good and cheap near the centre.
- Doddy says: *Finally. A problem worth solving. Opening Maps.*

- Card: Open in Maps — "cheap pizza near the centre" · Open in Maps / Not now
- Caption: Scripted demonstration. Opens Apple Maps with the search, which leaves the app.

### 15:30 · Explain it like I'm five. — Conversation · in testing
- Problem: A diagram with forty arrows. None of them pointing at an explanation.

- Card: Where this came from — Wikipedia · Quantum computing · looked up

### 18:45 · My brain has left the chat. — Saved notes · built
- Problem: You told someone about a restaurant last week. You told Doddy too, luckily.
- You say: What was that Italian place I mentioned last week?
- Doddy says: *Trattoria Nonna, Hoża 9. Your words, not mine.*
- Doddy does: Finds the note you saved, in your exact words. · Shows when you saved it and where it came from. · Says "I couldn't find that" instead of guessing.
- Card: Found in your notes — "Italian place for Ola's birthday: Trattoria Nonna, Hoża 9. Book the table by the window." · Saved 4 Oct 2026, 13:12 · Your typed note

### 23:30 · Sort tomorrow out. — Plans · in testing

- Caption: Scripted demonstration. One tick box per change; nothing unticked runs.
- Then (Doddy walks): *That's the day. Mostly.*

## Rules the lines follow

Answer first, one dry beat at most. Jokes land on the situation or on Doddy, never on the visitor (which is why "so no excuses" went). No feelings claimed. "Done" only with a receipt. Serious moments get no joke. (From `docs/product/PERSONALITY_SETTINGS.md`.)
