Three ways forward

A · Swap the homepage to the pack now recommended

  • One Doddy everywhere: the app, the new page, the homepage, the favicon.
  • The rule already says so (CLAUDE.md, 11 October) and the site README lists it as owed.
  • Eleven swaps, four of them exact, five nearest-with-a-prop, two waiting on cuts (basketball sheet, canvas).
  • Cost: an afternoon. Weight: SVGs of 6 KB become PNGs; at two sizes (320 and 640 px, WebP) about 25 to 60 KB each, so the homepage grows by roughly 350 KB, lazy-loaded below the hero.
  • Risk: the v1.3 backpack and basketball go; "orange backpack" in the Meet copy becomes a copy edit (proposed).

B · Keep both for now

  • Reason to: the v1.3 set is still a candidate brand master the owner has not formally retired, and the pack's README calls its poses concept art to be redrawn.
  • Cost: nothing today. Price: the new page launches with a different Doddy from the page it links from; the one contradiction every visitor sees.
  • Only defensible if the owner plans to retire the pack, not the SVGs.

C · Swap the new page to v1.3 instead

  • Also one Doddy, and the SVGs are lighter.
  • But it breaks the character-art rule, contradicts the app (where the pack is live), and the v1.3 set has no scenery, no props, no walk, run, jump or pop strips: the whole Phase 2 motion plan would go.
  • Not recommended.

Decision for the owner: A, B or C. If A: approve the five nearest-pose swaps and the two pending cuts (basketball sheet shooting frame; a pack pose for the court game). The hero in mockup 25a shows A.
