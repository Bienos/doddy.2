What's real today.

Doddy is in early testing. Here is what each part means.

  • Built

    Your notes

    Save a note in your words. Find it again. Correct it, export it, delete it.

  • In testing

    Talking to Doddy

    Voice, calendar, reminders, alarms, timers, lists, maps, weather, message drafts and plans. Tried on real iPhones.

  • Later, maybe

    Not built

    Translating other people, picking restaurants for you, Android. No dates promised.

In testing

Say any of it out loud.

Tap Talk to Doddy and ask. Doddy looks through your notes, and uses the calendar, reminders, lists and maps you've switched on in Settings.

Doddy asks before it saves or changes anything, and shows a receipt only once the change is confirmed.

What leaves your iPhone. Your notes stay on it. Voice (OpenAI, or ElevenLabs if you switch the voice), weather and Wikipedia lookups, shared lists (your iCloud) and connected accounts send data off it only when you use or switch them on. The app lists each one.

What changed and why

Each change maps to a finding in PROMPT_how-it-works-vs-the-day.md. Facts behind the proposed words still need the engineering check in that prompt.

  • Notes told once (R1, R2, S3)

    Step 2 owns "your words, not a guess" and the no-match line. Step 3 owns the receipt. The receipt section goes, and the 18:45 scene leaves the homepage teaser (it stays on the new page).

  • One list of what's not built (R4)

    "What's real today" is the only place that says Built, In testing and Later, maybe. The homepage's own "Later, maybe" paragraph goes, and page B's can link to this strip or repeat it word for word.

    Use the same three lines on both pages, from COPY.md "What's real today".

  • No false "all on your iPhone" (C1, C4)

    The step-list lede keeps what is true of notes only. The data line names every outside service, including weather and Wikipedia.

    "Your notes stay on your iPhone with no account." Drop "no setup".

  • Heading says the status (S2)

    The teaser's heading and lede now say "all in testing", so a visitor can't read three scenes as shipped. None is about notes, so nothing repeats step 1 to 3.

  • Voice scope matches page B (C3, S4)

    The voice section moves after the scenes and describes what the scenes show, not notes only.

    "Doddy asks before it saves or changes anything." Check this against the app first.

  • The 18:45 scene (C2)

    It is spoken, so it is voice, which is In testing, yet it is labelled Built. Either label it "Typed search: built · voice: in testing" on page B, or show it as the typed search in step 2.
