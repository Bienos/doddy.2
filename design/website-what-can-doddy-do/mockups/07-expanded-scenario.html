The awkward text.

Three drafts. Two deleted. One "hey" sent by accident.

  • Writes a draft in your voice, not his.
  • Three nudges: funnier, shorter, more confident.
  • Opens Messages with the draft. Sending stays your job.

Message drafts · in testing

I don't send. You send.

What each nudge does to the draft

Funnier selected
Draft to Alex

Alex, 10:04Yesterday was fun :)

Yesterday was fun. Same again next week? No pressure, my calendar is mostly staring at walls.

Open in Messages

Doddy's voice stays out of it: the joke is about your calendar, not about Alex.

Shorter selected
Draft to Alex

Alex, 10:04Yesterday was fun :)

Yesterday was fun. Next week?

Open in Messages

The card shrinks with the text (200 ms). Reduced motion: it just changes.

More confident selected
Draft to Alex

Alex, 10:04Yesterday was fun :)

Yesterday was fun. Let's do Thursday, same place. I'll book.

Open in Messages
