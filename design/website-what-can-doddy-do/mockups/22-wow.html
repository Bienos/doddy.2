What it does. The pill reads the visitor's clock (the browser's, nothing leaves the phone), Doddy's hero pose and line match the hour, and "Start the day" becomes "Start at the nearest moment". Six variants cover the clock: morning (yawn), late morning (wave), lunch (fist pump), afternoon (leaning), evening (cross-legged), small hours (shush). People screenshot the 3 a.m. one. Cost: an afternoon; six poses already here; six short lines in the proposed-copy list. Honesty: the pill says "where you are", never "I know where you are"; no location, no storage. Without JavaScript: the plain hero.

No share sheet (desktop)the link is copied and a "Copied" chip appears, the homepage's Saved chip

Where it sits. One handwritten line under each moment's label, in Doddy's voice, ten lines in all. The sign-off note is the homepage's receipt paper, taped under the 23:30 scene, before the honesty panel.

Why this one. Shareability that points at one moment, not the whole page: the link opens on that scene (the ring and the strip already make moments addressable), and the text is a line a friend would actually send. Cost: half a day: ten anchors, the Web Share call with a copy fallback, one note. Honesty: the share text repeats the moment's label; "in testing" rides along in the page it opens. Copy: ten "send" lines, one note and one text per moment are new words, in the proposed-copy list.

What lost, and why
  • Replay the whole day in ten seconds. Fun once, but it is the page played fast; the per-moment replay already exists and a friend gets sent a moment, not a page.
  • The homepage-to-page crossing (mockup 11). Kept as proposed; it is a transition, not a reason to share.
  • The loose ends come back as the plan card. Already the 23:30 moment; adding a callback animation would be a second thing happening at once.
  • The pigeon from the film. The pack has no pigeon, and drawing one is forbidden.
  • A "say it in Polish" toggle. Honest and on-brand, but ten lines of new Polish copy need approval first; proposed for Phase 3.
  • Doddy keeps up with your scroll speed. A game that fights the reading; and the budget says one thing at a time.
  • Pull down to wake him on phones. Phones only, once, and it collides with the browser's own pull-to-refresh.
  • Pick your three moments and get a card. The best runner-up: a canvas-composed image is a day's work and a share image needs the owner's sign-off on using pack art as an export.
