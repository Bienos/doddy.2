What moves, and what never does

Moves (transform, opacity)

  • Doddy: frame swaps from the pack's strips, plus translateX along a path.
  • Objects and props: translateY on drop-in, rotate for a rattle or a tip, scaleY for a page flip.
  • Bubbles and cards: scale .97 → 1 with opacity (the homepage's bubble-pop), translateY 24 px → 0.
  • Stamps: scale 1.3 → 1 with opacity.
  • The scribble underline: clip-path reveal (the homepage's own rule).
  • Effects (burst, puff, dash lines, question mark): opacity, sometimes scale.

Never moves

  • Text never translates. Doddy's line is revealed word by word with a clip, inside a bubble that has already landed.
  • Layout never shifts: every animated thing is absolutely positioned or animates inside its own box, so nothing below it jumps.
  • Scenery never pans or zooms. The camera is a notebook page.
  • Doddy is never tweened between drawings; between frames he is one frame or the next.
  • Colour never animates (no glow pulsing, no orange flashing).
  • Nothing moves on the honesty panel, the join panel or the footer.

Frames are the app's own cut-outs of the pack (the DoddyMotion image sets), so the website and the app move the same way. Each strip is one sprite image per size, swapped with steps(); a moment never needs more than ten frames.

Triggers, loading and the fallbacks
  • Scroll triggers use one IntersectionObserver: a card at 40% visibility starts its beats, a travel strip at 30% starts its walk, the day strip's current moment follows the card that is most in view. Each fires once; a flag per moment in memory, not in storage.
  • Preloading: when a moment reaches 60% the next moment's strip and poses are fetched, so a swap never shows a blank. Scenery is lazy-loaded one moment ahead.
  • Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): the page is beat 3 everywhere, the travels show their last frame, the surprise is a still, hover keeps its 150 ms, the replay button is hidden. The homepage's own rule (every transition and animation off) still applies on top.
  • No JavaScript: the page is beat 3 everywhere; the object ring is seven links; the day strip is a list of links. Nothing is hidden behind script.
  • Phones: same beats, same timings; entrances are shorter paths (60 px) because the card is narrower. Tap replaces hover in the hero (mockup 18).
  • Battery and data: frames are swapped, not decoded video; no requestAnimationFrame loop runs when nothing is animating; will-change: transform is set only for the 1.8 s a beat plays.
