# Motion v2: decisions already made

Where the brief’s numbers collided with each other or with the mock layout, and what the reference engine and the clips do. Each one is a setting in the reference engine. The owner can overrule any of them.

## Tab hop to Memories would land late

The brief’s flight formula gives 419 ms for Talk → Memories (336 pt, 326 pt drop), which lands 499 ms after the tap. Tab-hop flights are capped so contact is never later than 484 ms; the capped value shows in each clip’s measurements. Everything else uses the formula as written.

## Brace threshold

At 3,000 pt/s² he would brace on every ride: a normal review open starts near 60,000 pt/s² and averages about 16,000 over 100 ms, the keyboard about 14,000, a collapse about 19,000. Brace uses the 100 ms mean, on above 24,000 and off once below 8,000 for 120 ms, so ordinary opens and keyboard rides never brace and a flick (28,000–33,000) does. Both numbers are settings.

## A tap on him and the message field

The journey makes the field focusing its own beat after he lands, so a tap on him opens Talk without focusing the field. If the product focuses the field with the same tap, the keyboard starts in the commit frame and he aims at where the composer will be; the prototype has a switch for that version.

## Composer and keyboard

The composer moves with the keyboard’s own curve between its two resting places, the way the keyboard layout guide animates it, so it starts moving with the keyboard instead of when the keys reach it.

## Microphone button

To make “side-steps to keep Send clear” literal, the microphone takes the trailing slot and Send slides 44 pt toward him. His Talk spot then tops out at x 240 instead of 284. Check this against the real composer layout.

## “Review ›” hit area

At x 250 his clear zone ends at 274 pt, so “Review ›” has to start at 298 or later. The mock puts it at 300.

## Rapid switching while Talk is selected

The rail under Talk would stand him in front of the message field, so on Talk he waits on the composer edge instead. On Home and Memories he waits on the rail (or the strip) as written.

## Breathing after every landing

v1 study 11 (keep) puts three breaths after any settle, so he breathes on Talk and Memories too, not only on Home.

## N5 is 18.5 s

Three 4.2 s breaths starting 2 s after he settles, then sitting at 15 s idle, can’t fit in 8 s. It is one real-time take with no time-lapse.

## N13 joins 7 s into his idle

So the 10 s sit lands 3 s into the clip.

## Toast in N15

Ducking needs something moving within 12 pt above his head, so the toast slides across just above him and rests clear on the left. Where the real toast lives decides whether he ever ducks in practice.

## Not studied

Peek, perch on the wordmark, empty Memories and returning to the app keep their v1 behaviour and weren’t in this brief’s list of deliverables, so they have no clips and aren’t in the prototype.
