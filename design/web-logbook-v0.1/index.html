Closer look comes with a Doddy plan

At a glance stays free, with everything that's on it. Closer look adds:

  • Any stretch up to 90 days
  • Every tool counted, and how each one ended
  • When you talk, by weekday and hour
  • Notes, lists and handed-over tasks in detail

Your numbers keep being counted either way. Download everything and Delete stay free, and nothing is deleted if you don't subscribe.

What this page knows

On this page

  • How many times you talked, when, and for how long
  • Which of Doddy's tools ran, and how each one ended
  • How many notes you saved, how, and their type

Never on this page

  • Your notes, lists or tags
  • Anything you or Doddy said, or any audio
  • Names, places, emails or events
  • Private notes, not even a count

Yours to decide

  • Off until you turn it on in the app
  • Kept 90 days on Doddy's server in the EU, then deleted
  • Turning it off in the app deletes it here too
