## Remaining limits

The conversation is scripted, not AI. Free answers populate the current explicit field rather than being interpreted into hidden traits. Native durable profile storage, actual personalized model responses, language following, microphone/audio, reminders and background work are not implemented. Reload/reset clears this demo.

New routes are primarily English; full bilingual copy, VoiceOver and native Dynamic Type need review. Owner identity is not verified or inferred from the phone. No audience/usability outcome, legal approval, market validation, native build or physical-device result is asserted by these browser checks.
