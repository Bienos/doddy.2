# Design decisions and research

As of 8 October 2026. Design proposals, not evidence of customer demand.

## Recommendation

Pocket notebook gives the useful memory loop a strong visual identity without adding a metaphor the user has to learn. Native-style actions remain literal: save, find, inspect, correct. Doddy can carry a little card in the Home composition, but becomes smaller in conversation and disappears when a detail needs careful reading. Paper and ink create continuity across brand and product. The handwritten accent changes the rhythm; it does not replace readable instructions.

The design hypothesis is that a familiar, calm surface plus transparent source inspection can make explicit memory easier to understand. This has not been tested with users. The supplied research distinguishes follow-through, translation and memory as different candidate jobs; this study resolves the handoff's memory loop rather than declaring it the winning product strategy.

## Three comparable alternatives

| Direction | Hierarchy and rhythm | Signature interaction | Main tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pocket notebook | Handwritten display accent, short editorial lines, visible save/find actions, quiet recent-note row | A confirmed note becomes a receipt; recall opens its source | Too much handwriting would impair scanning, so only brief headings use it |
| Quiet companion | System-like typography, more central character space, rounded primary control, fewer competing visual cues | Character presence gives way to an uncluttered answer | Strong calm but lower discovery density; a long Home requires scrolling |
| Tiny investigation | More compact typography, numbered first step and an answer/source reading rail | Trace a useful claim directly to its saved clue | A process metaphor can add unnecessary steps; keep it optional |

The same fictional spare-key scenario appears in all three. Layout, scale and reading sequence change; it is not a palette exercise. All three use supplied poses unchanged.

## Small moments that carry the character

- Home: “A little less to carry.” A useful invitation, not a claim of human affection.
- Capture: “Something useful. In your own words.” Explicit authorship beats invisible inference.
- Save: “Kept for later.” Only after the mock receipt.
- Recall: “It’s in here somewhere.” A heading, followed by a real matching-candidate list in the demo.
- Correction: “A small correction.” No defensive performance; preserve the original.
- Failure: “That didn’t save.” State the result, preserve the draft, then offer retry.

These are proposed interface lines. They do not establish catchphrases or override future brand copy. Polish Home/capture/recall/detail samples are transcreated; other staged/failure copy is still English and needs bilingual review.

## Scope discipline

AI conversation and six voice states are clearly marked planned and simulated. Native permissions, audio and providers are not involved. Reminders and Live Activities were deliberately left out of the screen suite because no useful task beyond this memory loop requires them. Their engineering boundaries remain in the original handoff.

No market metric, retention effect, legal approval, price or launch claim follows from this design. The recommendation needs owner selection and observed usability testing before native integration.
