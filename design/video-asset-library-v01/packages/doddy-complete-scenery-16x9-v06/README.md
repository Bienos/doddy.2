# Doddy’s world — 44 scenes

28 everyday and seasonal backgrounds plus 16 European capitals, with open foregrounds for Doddy and friends.

All PNGs are opaque and exported at 1920 × 1080 (16:9). New generated images are resampled with macOS sips without cropping. Generated dimensions and final SHA-256 hashes are in manifest.json. Open index.html to browse; click a scene to open its full-resolution PNG.

Real cities use distinctive landmarks in simplified illustrative compositions. They are designed for visual recognition, not as literal georeferenced street views or maps. No city labels are printed in the artwork; filenames and gallery identify each place. AI-generated architectural details may differ from the real buildings. These are flat bitmap backgrounds, not layered scenes or editable vectors. Previous packs remain separate.
