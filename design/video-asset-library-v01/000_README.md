## How to use

The blink, hand, prop, effects and foreground PNGs have genuine alpha transparency. The other PNGs are flat reference sheets or backgrounds. All character sheets are illustration references to redraw/trace and rig; they are not separated animation layers, timed cycles, video clips or editable vectors.

Foreground extraction is generative and approximate: compare against the matching background and align/redraw edges before animation. Day/night layouts are close visual variants, not pixel-identical colour swaps. The pose keys need an animator to adjust spacing, proportions and motion continuity. Expression/gaze directions are stylised; some emotion distinctions are subtle. Generated paper/colour fills vary slightly rather than implementing exact brand tokens.

Portrait assets are approximately 9:16 (941 × 1672). The blink strip is 3:1 (2172 × 724), rather than the requested 4:1; place it on a wider transparent canvas in your animation tool if needed. Use the supplementary SVG guide when exact editorial margins matter: 14% top, 20% bottom/right, 5% left. The generated PNG guide is visual only.

## Remaining source-dependent assets

The three photo composites in sections 10.1–10.3 require the user's own desk/coffee/pocket photos; none were supplied. They have not been replaced with invented personal photos. Photography within storyboards and the caption sample is AI generated. The optional backpack turnaround was omitted; Doddy wears no backpack in this kit.

The keys scene and transitions are fictional character stories, not proof of object finding, memory, voice or system integration. This pack does not select or approve either conflicting v1.3 brand master, change the existing packs, or integrate assets into the native app.
