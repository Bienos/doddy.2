My job. By me.

Doddy wrote his own job description. We kept it as is.

Job description

Title
Mate. In your pocket.
Hours
Whenever you say my name. Or tap. I don't listen otherwise.
Main duties
Remember the small stuff in your exact words. Find it when you ask. Read your morning out loud. Draft the awkward text. Set the reminder, after you tap. Sort tomorrow. Tell you when it rains at four.
Skills
Saying "I don't know". Not guessing. One joke per problem, maximum.
Salary
A "cheers" now and then.

Not my job

  • Sending anything. You send.
  • Remembering things you didn't tell me.
  • Changing your calendar without a tap.
  • Having feelings about it. I'm an AI.
  • Making you feel bad for forgetting. That's what I'm for.
  • Doing the shopping. Yet.
