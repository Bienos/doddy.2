Build notes · not part of the page

Three ways to put the film under the hero

Recommended

A · Taped into the notebook

The film sits on a ruled notebook page with two strips of tape and a handwritten caption. Doddy points at it from the margin. The card starts slightly tilted and straightens when it scrolls into view.

  • Closest to the Pocket Notebook look
  • Lightest to build: no scroll maths
  • Works the same on phones
Most cinematic

B · Cinema reveal

The film starts as an inset rounded card and opens to full width as you scroll. Good for a launch moment. It needs more QA on phones and a reduced-motion fallback, which this mock includes.

  • Strongest first impression
  • Pins the page for one extra screen of scroll
Most useful

C · Chapter notebook

Seven chapters cut at the film's own scene changes. Each one jumps to its moment. On phones the list becomes a swipeable strip. Rename the chapters to match the voice-over.

  • Lets people skip to the end card
  • Chapter titles here are placeholders
Placement
Landing page only, the first section after the hero. Hero is sized to leave the top of the film visible above the fold.
Autoplay
Muted, looping 9-second preview while in view. Pauses off screen. Off when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
Sound
Only after a tap. Restarts from 0:00 with sound and native controls.
Captions
Add a WebVTT file (<track kind="captions">) once the voice-over script is final.
Source
DODDY-LOW_RES.mov · HEVC 854×480 · 1:20 · 17.7 MB
Web file
H.264 MP4, faststart · 5.6 MB (this mock). Ship a 1080p master as H.264 plus an AV1/WebM copy.
Why re-encode
HEVC in .mov does not play in Chrome or Firefox on most desktops.

This is a design mockup. There is no Doddy website in the repository yet, so the landing page around the film is a stand-in built from the Pocket Notebook direction (paper, ink, restrained orange, handwritten accents). The headline borrows the film's end card, "A mate in your pocket." Nav links and the beta button go nowhere.
