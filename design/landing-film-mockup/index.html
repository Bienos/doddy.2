Mockup notes, not part of the site

Three ways to put the film under the hero

Recommended

A · Notebook film

The film is taped onto a paper card that straightens as it scrolls in. Doddy dribbles in from the corner and says "That's me." in the same handwriting as the hero. The chapter stickers reuse the "small things" scraps from the next section, and each one jumps to its moment.

Most cinematic

B · Cinema reveal

The film starts as an inset rounded card on ruled paper and opens to the full width of the screen as you scroll. It's the strongest launch moment, but it holds the page for about one extra screen. People who prefer reduced motion get a plain, static player.

Most playful

C · Postcard

The film is the front of a postcard from Warsaw, with a handwritten note, a Doddy stamp and a postmark on the back. It's charming and on-brand, but the handwriting adds copy that has to stay short.

Only after a tap. The film restarts from 0:00 with native controls.
File
Your .mov is HEVC, which most desktop Chrome and Firefox can't play. This mock uses a 5.6 MB H.264 MP4. Ship H.264 plus an AV1 WebM, with preload="metadata" and a poster.
Captions
Add a WebVTT captions track once the voice-over script is final.
