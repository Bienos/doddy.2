Deleting a remembered conversation can now delete the recap note made from it. Recaps saved from now on are linked to their conversation by its id (`RecapLinks`, UserDefaults `recaps.links.v1`; no database migration). Deleting a conversation in Settings asks: delete the conversation and its recap note, or only the conversation. The history file is rewritten first; the conversation is then tombstoned, so a recap still being written is dropped (or, if it was already being saved, deleted again). "Forget remembered conversations" tombstones every kept conversation but keeps saved recap notes, as its copy says.
Notes saved before this change have no link and are never matched by date or wording: they stay until the person deletes them. Copies on Doddy's server or ElevenLabs are not touched. If the recap note can't be deleted the screen says so and the note stays linked. Account isolation is still not implemented.
Code: `RecapLinks.swift` (DoddyCore), `ConversationDeletion.swift`, `RecapKeeper.recapConversation(conversationID:)`, `RememberedConversationsView`.
