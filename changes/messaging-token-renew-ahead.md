iPhone: the messaging-server device token is renewed by rotation before it runs out, so the first call after its end no longer fails once. Step 5 of (e) in `2026-11-12-account-and-device-tokens`, using `POST /v1/accounts/sign-in` with the current token (live since #476).
How: the token's `expiresAt` is kept next to it (`MessagingTokenRecord`, which names its token by a hash and holds no token; a token with no known end counts as "renew at the next chance"). At launch and when Doddy comes to the front, a token within 14 days of its end is rotated, at most once a day, one at a time, with the account the app is signed in to and without asking. 14 days: at least two weeks of daily chances to get through an offline or unopened stretch, while a token is still rotated only about every 166 days (`MessagingTokenRenewal.renewWindow`, a constant, not a setting). Offline or any failure keeps the old token; a refused account token is refreshed and asked once more, then left to the existing refusal path; an answer for another account, or for an account that changed meanwhile, is not stored and is handed to be ended on the server. The new token is stored before it is used and the old one is not kept.
