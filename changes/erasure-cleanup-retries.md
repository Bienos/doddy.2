Backend, account deletion (ADR-674, ADR-709): erasure clean-ups keep retrying and report when stuck. Removals owed at a provider are retried by the hourly sweeps with a growing wait (5 minutes doubling, at most a day) and no last try; after 8 failures, or at once with no remover on the server, a `cleanup_stuck` warning and `stuck_at` (list: `npm run cleanups:stuck`). New `cleanups` columns `next_try_at`, `stuck_at`.
A request let in before a deletion can't write the person's rows back after it (401, checked against the deletion's cut-off row in each write's transaction). The account-link count uses a `day:` period and prune drops old `hour:` rows. Doddy Web: only wrong codes count toward the server-wide hour, and a right code still signs in when it is spent.
