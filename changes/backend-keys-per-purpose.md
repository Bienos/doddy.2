Backend: session tokens and file links are signed with a key of their own per job, derived from `DODDY_SESSION_SIGNING_KEY` with HKDF-SHA256, and carry a key id (`backend/src/signingKeys.ts`). Optional `DODDY_SESSION_SIGNING_KEY_PREVIOUS` is accepted for checking, never for signing; optional `DODDY_IDENTIFIER_KEY` keeps each iPhone's user id and the invite-code counter ids where they are (unset: the signing key itself, as before), and a previous key without an identifier key is refused at start. Same two names and meanings as the voice token server's change (#475). Tokens and links made before this change keep verifying, so no setting changes at deploy and nobody is signed out. Decision `2026-11-14-backend-keys-per-purpose` (step b and step 1 of e of `2026-11-12-account-and-device-tokens`); rotation recipe in `docs/integrations/DEPLOY_RENDER.md`.
Rollback: reverting leaves earlier tokens and links valid; tokens and links made by this build are not accepted by the earlier code, so after a rollback they stop working until they would have expired (sessions up to 30 days, links a day). Device sessions sign in again by themselves on a 401; a token made with `session:mint` is made again.
Ran: `backend/test/signing-keys.test.ts` (red first), `scripts/ci.sh --only backend`, `scripts/ci.sh --only checks`. Not run: against Render or a device.
Deploy: nothing to set. Rotating later is the owner's step (recipe above). The same change for the voice server is step 2.
