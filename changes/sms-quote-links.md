Messaging server: words that are not the person's never reach their phone as a link from Doddy's number. One shared `withoutLinks` (`src/untrustedWords.ts`) now removes links with any scheme, `mailto:`/`tel:`, bare addresses on any ending (`goo.gl/x`, `evil.de`), IPv4, e-mail addresses and markdown/anchor wrappers, also when written with wide letters, other dots or invisible characters. It is used for the quote and the place's name in a call task's "text me the result", and for the answer to a text in Text with Doddy (a link becomes "[link pominięty]"). The docs now say that the sender number of a text can't be proven by code.
Ran: `scripts/ci.sh --only messaging` against a local Postgres 16 (all 1,241 tests pass, none skipped) and `scripts/ci.sh --only checks` (all pass). Not run: a real text through Twilio or a real call. Reaches users only when the owner deploys `doddy-messaging` (`docs/OWNER_QUEUE.md` 90).
