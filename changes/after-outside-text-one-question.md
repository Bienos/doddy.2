# After an email, a page or the screen: one spoken question, and "tak" is enough

Owner, 15 Nov 2026: "2". Decision `2026-11-15-after-outside-text-one-question`.
- **Before a change:** once Doddy has read something from outside in a conversation, it asks once, and the person's own yes or a tap runs the change. Until now a tap was needed.
- **Doddy's own things:** a note, a reminder, a timer, a list or a draft no longer happens at once there; it waits for that yes.
- **Lists:** items for any list ask, unless the person just said every item.
- **Unchanged:** money and bookings stay a tap. Without outside text, nothing changes. The stopwatch, focus, wind-down, the translator and following news still start at once.
- **Ran:** DoddyCore tests, `scripts/ci.sh --only checks,swift`. **Not run:** the app build (CI `ios-build`), a device.
- **Owner step / live agent:** sync the live agent's prompt line and six tool descriptions after a build with this is on TestFlight.
