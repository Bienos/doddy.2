# Studio: the job's dollar limit is enforced before each call
Studio's `STUDIO_JOB_LIMIT_USD` was checked only after a model call, so one call (up to 32k/64k output tokens) could cross it: with the fake provider and a $0.05 limit the job stopped only at ~$0.204 (reproduced first, test `studioBudget.test.ts`). Now each paid call gets a worst-case ceiling (prompt estimate, cache-write price, searches, `max_tokens` at the output price), `max_tokens` is clamped to the remaining budget (no call is sent if even a minimal reply doesn't fit: `cost_limit`), the ceiling is reserved and settled from reported usage, and an unknown-outcome call keeps 50% of its ceiling counted. Day/month reservations unchanged. Docs: `server/messaging/README.md` "Pre-call job budget".
With `main`'s unknown-cost handling (ADR-742): a call that was sent and ended without its usage sets `costUnknown` (the worker keeps the job's whole reservation on the day and month, as before) and also keeps half its ceiling counted against the job's own limit, so the job can't plan past it. `costUsd` therefore includes that allowance for such a job: it is an allowance, not a measured cost.
Price table (checked against Anthropic's pricing on 9 Nov and again against the claude-api skill's table on 14 Nov 2026): Opus 5.5 $4/$20, Sonnet 5.5 $2/$10, Haiku 5.5 $0.10/$0.50 (>100K prompt $0.50/$2.50), Fable 5.1 $10/$50; cache write 1.25x, reads 0.05x (Fable 0.025x, Haiku 0.1x); web search $10 per 1,000: all match `models.ts`, no change. Studio's models are unchanged.
Ran (14 Nov 2026, local Postgres 16): `scripts/ci.sh --only messaging` (`npm test` in `server/messaging`): 1,273 tests, 1,273 pass, 0 fail, 0 skipped; `scripts/ci.sh --only checks`: pass. `studio.test.ts` changed in two places: the "spending past the reservation" test now makes the plan reply larger than its ceiling (a call that fits can no longer overspend by itself, which is what this change is for), and a new test covers a limit that can't pay for even the first call (nothing sent, cost 0, the reservation back whole). Not run: a live Anthropic call (the ceiling's input estimate and the research tool-content allowance are estimates; check them on the next paid run). Deploy: `doddy-messaging` takes it when next deployed from `main`; no migration, no setting.
