Backend: the server now stores each iPhone that signs in with its device id (`devices`: a random row id, its own user id, first and last seen, when revoked; no token or content), lists an account's iPhones (`GET /v1/web/devices`) and revokes one (`DELETE /v1/web/devices/:id`, answered only after the store confirmed it; unknown or another account's id is a plain 404). A revoke ends that iPhone's tokens on the next request and no one else's: a device token made for a stored iPhone carries one more signed claim, `dev` (the signing is unchanged). A join to another account (ADR-819) ends after `DODDY_DEVICE_JOIN_DAYS` days (default 90, a plain number) unless the iPhone signs in again with the same Apple or Google, and unlinking a sign-in ends every join made with it. Decision `2026-11-14-stored-devices` (part c and step 3 of e of `2026-11-12-account-and-device-tokens`); the endpoints are in `docs/platforms/web.md`, the setting in `docs/integrations/DEPLOY_RENDER.md` and `backend/.env.example`.
No one is signed out at deploy: tokens made before this change keep working, every iPhone that already joined an account gets its row at the first start and its 90 days count from then, and the owner's iPhone and guests get a row at their next sign-in (within 28 days). What a person sees after a revoke or an ended join is in the decision file (read from the app code, not run on a device).
Rollback: tables and columns are additive and ignored by the earlier code; while it runs, revokes and join ends are not enforced (a revoked iPhone's tokens work and it can sign in again), and they apply again when this version runs again.
Ran: `backend/test/stored-devices.test.ts` (new, 27 tests; compile-red first, plus ten mutation checks), `scripts/ci.sh --only backend`, `--only checks`, `--only node` (`DODDY_DEVICE_JOIN_DAYS` is in `secrets/manifest.json` `notSecrets`). Not run: against Render or a device.
Deploy: `doddy` has auto-deploy off, so nothing is live until the owner deploys; no setting to set. The iPhone and web screens for the list are later pull requests.
