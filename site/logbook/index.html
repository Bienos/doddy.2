What Logbook will know

On the page

  • How many times you talked, when, and for how long
  • Which of Doddy's tools ran, and how each one ended
  • How many notes you saved, how, and their type

Never on the page

  • Your notes, lists or tags
  • Anything you or Doddy said, or any audio
  • Names, places, emails or events
  • Private notes, not even a count

Yours to decide

  • Off until you turn it on in the app
  • Kept 90 days on Doddy's server, then deleted
  • Turning it off in the app deletes it here too
