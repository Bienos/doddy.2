The agent you'd let near your inbox.

Everyone's building agents that book flights while you sleep. Only 9% of people would let one buy something unchecked. Doddy is built for the other 91%.

Most agents

  • Act, then tell you.
  • Send the email.
  • Remember everything, including what you didn't say.
  • Sound sure when they aren't.
  • Live in the cloud, with your stuff.

Doddy

  • Shows the card, waits for your tap.
  • Drafts the email. You send it.
  • Keeps only what you told him, in your words, with the date.
  • Says "I don't know" out loud.
  • Keeps your notes on your iPhone. Says what leaves it, and when.

Lost your phone? Borrow one. Call him.

Doddy gets a number. Any phone, your PIN, and he reads your notes back. A new one waits in the app for you to confirm.

Your notes, your call.

  • Stored on your iPhone

    Notes are stored in the app on your phone, protected by iOS. They leave it only for things you switch on, and the app lists each one under What leaves this iPhone.

  • No account

    No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and start.

  • Export any time

    Take everything with you as JSON or plain text.

  • Delete for real

    Delete one note or all of them. They're gone from search straight away.

Doddy is an AI, not a person. Some things you switch on send data off the phone: voice conversations (OpenAI, or ElevenLabs if you switch the voice), weather and Wikipedia lookups (a place name or search words), phone calls and memory on calls (Doddy's server, Twilio and ElevenLabs), shared lists (your iCloud), and connected accounts like Gmail or Todoist (through Doddy's server). Everything else stays on your iPhone. All of it is in early testing.
