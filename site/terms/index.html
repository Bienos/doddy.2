7. What Doddy asks before doing

These are the rules Doddy is built to follow; the app checks them before a tool runs:

  • Reading your own things (notes, calendar, contacts, photos, steps, email, files and the like): Doddy says what it wants to look at and waits for your yes, spoken or tapped.
  • Changing, sending, deleting or sharing something (an event, a reminder, a list item, an email, a task): only after you tap a card that shows exactly what will happen. A spoken yes is not enough for these.
  • Public look-ups (weather, Wikipedia, web, news and place searches, sums and conversions) run straight away, without asking. They don't read your things or change anything; only the words searched for leave your iPhone.
  • A task you hand over to Doddy (where this is available): one yes covers all the web searches and page reads for that one task. Doddy can't book, buy, send or start anything for it.
  • Money: Doddy never sends money. For a bank transfer it shows a card that opens your bank's app, where you finish (or don't).

Please read a card before you tap it. Some actions can't be undone; for example, an email that has been sent can't be unsent.

  • If you make an encrypted backup, keep the passphrase. We don't have it and can't recover it.
  • Keep copies of anything important. Your notes live on your iPhone, and you can export them in Settings.

16. Stopping and ending

You can stop at any time

You don't need to tell us. To remove your data as well:

  • Settings › Delete all memories removes your notes and lists from this iPhone;
  • Settings › Voice › Delete Doddy voice account ends your voice sign-in on Doddy's server (to remove Doddy from Sign in with Apple, also do that in your Apple ID settings);
  • Settings › Notifications and texts › Delete my Doddy messaging data deletes what Doddy's messaging server holds, if you used it;
  • Disconnect each online account in Settings › Online accounts;
  • then delete the app.

Some copies are not removed this way, for example your iPhone or iCloud backups, files you exported, and what the voice services keep. The Privacy Policy lists them and how to ask about them.

When we may limit or end your access

We may limit, pause or end your use of Doddy's servers if you break these terms, if the law requires it, or if we stop the service. Where we can, we will tell you first and give you time to export your notes (they are on your iPhone, so they stay with you).
