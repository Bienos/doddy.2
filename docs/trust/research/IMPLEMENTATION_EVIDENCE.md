# Evidence and sources used to frame the constitution
Date verified: 2026-10-10. This is not a claim that product features are implemented or validated.

## Standards / official guidance
- OWASP LLM06:2025 Excessive Agency — https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/ . Explains how excessive functionality, permissions and autonomy cause damaging tool executions; advocates least privilege and approvals.
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework — https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework . Voluntary framework addressing reliability, accountability, safety and privacy.
- NIST Generative AI Profile — https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence . Generative AI risk identification and management.
- OpenAI, Expanding on what we missed with sycophancy — https://openai.com/index/expanding-on-sycophancy/ . Official retrospective of overagreeable responses and need for behavioural review.
- Apple, App Review Guidelines — https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ . Permission, data use and disclosure requirements.
- Apple, Purpose Strings guidance — https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-purpose-strings . Permission descriptions must be clear and specific.
