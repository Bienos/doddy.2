## B. FA(3): what matters for a simple domestic invoice from a sole trader

All rows are from the brochure (Aug 2026) and the XSD unless another source is given.

| # | Field / rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Mandatory elements: `Naglowek`, `Podmiot1`, `Podmiot2`, `Fa`. Optional: `Podmiot3`, `Stopka`, `Zalacznik`, `PodmiotUpowazniony`. Header: `KodFormularza` = FA (3) with wersjaSchemy 1-0E, `WariantFormularza` = 3, and `DataWytworzeniaFa` (a datetime that may differ from P_1). | [V] |
| B2 | **Podmiot1 (seller):** NIP, Nazwa and Adres (KodKraju + AdresL1) are mandatory in the XSD. | [V] XSD |
| B3 | **Podmiot2 buyer identity** is a choice of exactly one: `NIP` for a Polish taxpayer, *or* `KodUE` + `NrVatUE` (for WDT / art. 100(1)(4) services / triangulation), *or* `KodKraju` (optional) + `NrID` for another tax ID, *or* `BrakID` = 1 when there is no ID (for example a **consumer**). A Polish NIP must go in `NIP`, never in NrVatUE/NrID, or the buyer will not get the invoice in KSeF. | [V] |
| B4 | `Podmiot2/Nazwa` and `Podmiot2/Adres` are **optional in the XSD**. Legally they may be left out only on a simplified invoice ≤ 450 PLN (art. 106e ust. 5 pkt 3, `RodzajFaktury` = UPR). Leaving them out otherwise is "technicznie możliwe" but legally wrong. Adres = `KodKraju` + `AdresL1` (≤ 512 chars), `AdresL2` optional. Consumer example (brochure Przykład 4): `BrakID` = 1, Nazwa, Adres, JST = 2, GV = 2. | [V] |
| B5 | inFakt says KSeF rejection happens when the counterparty's company name, full address or NIP is missing. This is stricter than the XSD; it is inFakt's statement for company buyers. | [V] pomoc.infakt.pl …/33043313615250 (2026-08-21) |
| B6 | **`JST` and `GV` are mandatory** (XSD minOccurs 1). Allowed values: "1" = the invoice concerns a subordinate JST unit / a VAT-group member, "2" = it does not. With "1", `Podmiot3` must also be filled, with role 8 (JST odbiorca) or role 10 (GV odbiorca) plus a NIP or IDWew. For an ordinary buyer both are **"2"**. | [V] |
| B7 | **P_1** is the issue date (see A13–A15). **P_2** is the seller's sequential invoice number, which is a different number from the KSeF number. In inFakt it is generated from "Następny numer faktury" unless `number` is sent. | [V] brochure; [V] docs.infakt.pl Invoice definition |
| B8 | **P_6** (sale/delivery date) is filled only "o ile taka data jest określona i różni się od daty wystawienia faktury". It sits in an optional XSD choice with `OkresFa` (P_6_Od/P_6_Do, for continuous services). Use `FaWiersz/P_6A` when lines have different dates. | [V] |
| B9 | **FaWiersz** has `NrWierszaFa` (mandatory). `P_7` name (≤ 512) is required except for a period-discount correction. `P_8A` unit (≤ 50 chars) and `P_8B` quantity (up to 6 decimals) are required except on a UPR ≤ 450 PLN invoice. `P_9A` unit net price (up to 8 decimals), `P_11` line net value and `P_12` rate are required except on UPR / margin schemes. All of these are minOccurs 0 in the XSD; the law makes them required. | [V] |
| B10 | **P_12 allowed values (XSD enum, complete):** `23`, `22`, `8`, `7`, `5`, `4`, `3`, `0 KR` (0 % domestic), `0 WDT`, `0 EX`, `zw`, `oo` (domestic reverse charge), `np I` (outside Poland, except art. 100(1)(4) and OSS), `np II` (art. 100(1)(4) services to EU taxpayers). | [V] XSD `TStawkaPodatku` |
| B11 | Totals per rate: P_13_1/P_14_1 (23/22), P_13_2/P_14_2 (8/7), P_13_3/P_14_3 (5), P_13_4 (taxi flat rate), P_13_6_1 (0 KR), P_13_6_2 (0 WDT), P_13_6_3 (0 EX), P_13_7 (zw), P_13_8 (np I), P_13_9 (np II), P_13_10 (oo), P_13_11 (margin). **P_15** (gross total) is mandatory. `KodWaluty` is mandatory (ISO 4217, "PLN"). | [V] |
| B12 | **"zw"** requires `Adnotacje/Zwolnienie/P_19` = 1 **and one of** `P_19A` (Polish statutory article, e.g. "Art. 43 ust. 1 pkt 37 ustawy…"), `P_19B` (Directive 2006/112 article) or `P_19C` (other legal basis). Without an exemption, `P_19N` = 1. | [V] |
| B13 | **"oo"** is domestic reverse charge (e.g. art. 145e: gas, electricity, emission allowances). It requires `P_18` = 1 (adnotacja "odwrotne obciążenie") and the sum in P_13_10; no VAT amount is shown. The 20 Oct 2026 draft extends art. 145e-type reverse charge to 30 Jun 2030. | [V] brochure; [V] gov.pl 20 Oct 2026 |
| B14 | **P_18A (MPP)** = 1 when the gross total exceeds **15 000 PLN** (or its equivalent in foreign currency) **and** the invoice covers goods or services from **annex 15**, supplied to a taxpayer; otherwise "2". The invoice must carry the words "mechanizm podzielonej płatności". Optional per-line flag: `P_12_Zal_15` = 1 for an annex-15 item, whether or not MPP is mandatory. | [V] |
| B15 | Other mandatory annotation flags, each "1" or "2": `P_16` (metoda kasowa), `P_17` (samofakturowanie), `P_18` (odwrotne obciążenie), `P_18A` (MPP), `P_23` (simplified triangulation). Also `Zwolnienie` (P_19… or P_19N), `NoweSrodkiTransportu` (P_22 or P_22N) and `PMarzy`. | [V] XSD |
| B16 | `RodzajFaktury` values: VAT, KOR, ZAL, ROZ, UPR, KOR_ZAL, KOR_ROZ. | [V] |
| B17 | **Platnosc is optional ("fakultatywny")**. It contains `TerminPlatnosci/Termin` (date) or `TerminOpis`, `FormaPlatnosci` (1 cash, 2 card, 3 voucher, 4 cheque, 5 credit, 6 transfer, 7 mobile) or `PlatnoscInna` + `OpisPlatnosci`, and `RachunekBankowy` (`NrRB` 10–34 chars mandatory inside it; SWIFT, NazwaBanku, OpisRachunku and RachunekWlasnyBanku optional). Also `Zaplacono`/`DataZaplaty`, part payments, `Skonto`, `LinkDoPlatnosci`, `IPKSeF`. | [V] |
| B18 | **Rounding:** KSeF does no arithmetic checks and will **accept** an invoice with wrong sums ("KSeF nie dokonuje obliczeń matematycznych"); the error must then be corrected with a correction invoice. Per-line VAT is legal: `P_11Vat` with art. 106e ust. 10 allows the total VAT to be the sum of line VAT amounts. Otherwise VAT is computed per rate on P_13_x. | [V] Podręcznik cz. II §1.4.2; brochure P_11Vat |
| B19 | **Limits:** FaWiersz ≤ **10 000** lines. Text fields ≤ 256 chars by default; P_7, Nazwa, AdresL1/L2 ≤ 512; Jednostka/PKWiU/CN/Indeks ≤ 50; GTIN ≤ 20. Amounts take 2 decimals (no thousands separator; "." as decimal point); P_9A/P_9B/P_10 up to 8 decimals; P_8B and KursWaluty up to 6. Podmiot3 ≤ 100. File ≤ **1 MB** without attachment, ≤ 3 MB with one. ≤ 10 000 invoices per session. UTF-8 without BOM. | [V] brochure; [V] CIRFMF weryfikacja-faktury.md (9 Apr 2026) |
| B20 | **Other KSeF checks:** duplicate = seller NIP + RodzajFaktury + P_2, **error 440** (uniqueness kept for 10 years). NIP **checksum** of Podmiot1/2/3 is verified on **production only**. | [V] CIRFMF weryfikacja-faktury.md |
| B21 | **Zalacznik** (attachment) is only for invoices with complex quantity, unit or price data, needs **prior notification in e-Urząd Skarbowy**, is sent in batch sessions only, and must not carry marketing or contract content. Not needed for Doddy. | [V] brochure; CIRFMF |
| B22 | **KSeF number:** 35 chars, `NIP(10)-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXXXXXXXX(12 hex)-CC(CRC-8)`, e.g. `5265877635-20250826-0100001AF629-AF`. The date part is the date of acceptance. The number is **not** in the invoice XML; it is returned in the **UPO** (Urzędowe Poświadczenie Odbioru). The UPO also shows the issue date (date of sending). | [V] github.com/CIRFMF/ksef-docs/blob/main/faktury/numer-ksef.md (n/d); najczestsze-pytania Q7 (15 Apr 2026); Podręcznik cz. II §1.4 |
| B23 | **QR "kod weryfikujący" (KOD I):** mandatory on every visualisation given to a buyer outside KSeF (consumer, foreign buyer, buyer without NIP) or used outside KSeF. The link is `https://qr.ksef.mf.gov.pl/invoice/{sellerNIP}/{P_1 as DD-MM-RRRR}/{SHA-256 of the invoice XML file, Base64URL}` (test host: `qr-test.ksef.mf.gov.pl`). The QR must follow ISO/IEC 18004:2024, with the **KSeF number printed under it**. Before a KSeF number exists (offline modes), print 2 codes: KOD I labelled "OFFLINE" and KOD II labelled "CERTYFIKAT", which needs a type-2 KSeF certificate. | [V] ksef.podatki.gov.pl/…/kody-weryfikujace-qr/ ; github.com/CIRFMF/ksef-docs kody-qr.md (21 Aug 2025) |

## Top 10 things the app must do for KSeF

1. **Never put a future issue date.** Create the invoice and send it to KSeF on the same Polish calendar day, with `invoice_date` = today. A future P_1 is rejected (A14). A past P_1 makes it offline24, with a next-business-day deadline (A13, A15, A8).
2. **Treat a KSeF `success` as permanent.** There is no edit, cancel or delete (A11). The only undo is a correction (C20, D15), so the confirmation screen before sending is the last safe moment. Never call DELETE on an invoice that has a `ksef_number` (C8 is unconfirmed).
3. **Send money in integer grosze** (`unit_net_price`, `net_price`, `gross_price`) and dates as `invoice_date` / `sale_date` / `payment_date` in RRRR-MM-DD (C1, C2). Update INFAKT_STATUS.md, which still marks these [U].
4. **Send to KSeF only on explicit confirmation.** Use `send_to_ksef: true` at create, or `POST /api/v3/ksef2/documents/{uuid}/send.json` afterwards. inFakt does not auto-send (C6, C9). The key needs `api:invoices:write` (C12). Use the `/api/v3` base (C13).
5. **Poll status until it is final.** Statuses are pending → sent → success / error, plus rejected. Only `success` with a `ksef_number` is "done" (C10). On `error` or `rejected`, show `status_description`. On an unknown outcome or "duplikat", don't resend; check status (C4, C19).
6. **Fill the buyer by type.** For a Polish firm: NIP of 10 digits without "PL", name, full address (B3–B5, C19). For a consumer: no NIP and `private_person` (B3, D9). For an EU or foreign buyer: VAT ID with country code, `client_country` and `sale_type` (D1, D2).
7. **Only offer VAT rates inFakt documents** (23/8/5/0/zw/np/oo) (C15). For `zw`, require an exemption id from `/vat_exemptions.json` (C16, B12). Block 0 % and np for foreign buyers until inFakt's mapping to `0 WDT` / `0 EX` / `np I` / `np II` is verified in the sandbox (C15 [U]).
8. **Ask about split payment** when gross > 15 000 PLN and an annex-15 item is present. Then set `split_payment: true` with `split_payment_type: "required"` (B14, C17).
9. **Validate locally before sending.** Check the NIP checksum (B20), "Uwagi" ≤ 256 chars, no `& < >` or double spaces in names, an uppercase SWIFT (C19), and that the bank account is already verified in inFakt (C18). For a foreign currency, require `vat_exchange_date_kind` (D4).
10. **Give consumers and foreign buyers a visualisation with KOD I.** It needs the QR code plus the KSeF number (B23, A7). Whether inFakt's PDF does this is unconfirmed (C23), so verify it in the sandbox before telling the user the PDF is compliant. Remember that B2C sending is optional (A6), and the ≤ 10 000 PLN/month exemption ends on 31 Dec 2026 (A2).
