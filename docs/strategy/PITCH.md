# Doddy pitch

*Rewritten 19 Oct 2026 for the sprawa direction (ADR-540).*

**Not usable yet as a "here's what it does" pitch.** The sprawa loop isn't joined up in the app and has never run on a device (`sprawa-loop.md`). Until the beta build passes the device checklist (Phase 2):
- use the **"what I'm building"** version below;
- never do the live demo;
- never say users, retention or a waitlist count until they exist in writing.

Re-check before each use: "Siri doesn't speak Polish" was true in June 2026 (Spider's Web, 8 Jun 2026).

## The 1-minute pitch, "what I'm building" version (true today)

> Quick question. Co ci wisi nad głową? The shoes you have to return by Friday? The dentist you promised to call back? The photos you swore you'd send your sister?
>
> We all carry a pile of small sprawy. Reminders come back at the wrong moment with no idea what they're about, and notes to ourselves never come back at all. So things slip.
>
> I'm building Doddy, a pomocnik in your pocket. You hand it a sprawa in one sentence, in Polish. It keeps your exact words, asks you one thing, when should I bring this back, and comes back at that moment with the next step ready. It does nothing without your tap. When you're done, one more tap: Gotowe.
>
> Sharp online. Clumsy offline. Honest always.
>
> Doddy already talks and keeps notes on my iPhone. Now I'm joining up the rest, and I need twenty people in Poland to break it for two weeks.
>
> Join the beta. Tell Doddy what's hanging over you.

*About 150 words.* "Talks and keeps notes on my iPhone" is the recorded part: a voice conversation on 11 Oct that saved and found notes (`STATUS.md`). Reminders and Gotowe are built but not recorded on a device; if asked, say exactly that.

## The 1-minute pitch, "here's what it does" version (only after the device checklist passes)

Same as above, with two changes:
- "I'm building Doddy" becomes "I built Doddy".
- "Doddy already talks and keeps notes on my iPhone. Now I'm joining up the rest, and I need" becomes "It works on my iPhone, and I need".

## Two openings
- **Funny, in Doddy's voice:** "Hi, I'm Doddy. Sharp online, clumsy offline. Give me your sprawy, I'll trip over everything else."
- **Serious:** "The things that slip aren't big. A return, a bill, a call you promised to make."

## Three tailored versions (only the hook and the ask change)

### (a) Investor or accelerator
- **Hook:** "Every big AI company is building an agent that does everything. Almost nobody trusts one to act alone. Doddy starts with the small sprawy, and never acts without a tap."
- **Ask:** "I'm not raising today. I'm running a two-week private beta with twenty people to prove they hand Doddy a sprawa and close it when it comes back. If the numbers clear the bar I've set, I'd like 20 minutes with you."

### (b) Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs host or programme
- **Hook:** "I spent four years on the commercial side of tech, then learned to build with AI agents. Doddy is what I built."
- **Ask:** "I'm looking for a host who has grown a consumer app, to learn how to turn beta testers into people who stay. And I'd love you to be one of my first testers too."

### (c) A potential beta tester
- **Hook:** "Co ci teraz wisi nad głową? Thought so."
- **Ask:** "Bring one real sprawa and try Doddy for two weeks. Then give me 15 minutes to tell me what annoyed you."

## The 15-second version
> "Doddy is a pomocnik in your iPhone. Hand it a sprawa in one sentence, in Polish. It keeps your exact words, brings it back at the right moment with the next step ready, and does nothing without your tap. Sharp online, clumsy offline, honest always. Join the beta at doddy.lol."

## Live demo (only after the device checklist passes; about 30 seconds)
1. **Before going on stage:** hand Doddy a sprawa with a reminder two minutes ahead, so the notification lands during the talk.
2. **On stage, say:** "Muszę oddać buty na Zalando do piątku." Doddy repeats your words and asks "Mam ci przypomnieć w czwartek wieczorem?" Tap yes. Hold up the card.
3. **When the prepared notification lands,** show it. Tap Gotowe, then show it logged as done, with Undo.
4. **The honesty beat:** ask for something Doddy can't do ("Zamów mi taksówkę"). It says it can't. That refusal is the demo.

**Backup:** voice sessions sometimes start twice or go silent (known bug, Phase 2). Keep a screen recording of the same demo. If voice hesitates for more than 3 seconds, type the sprawa instead and say "voice is in testing", which is true and on brand.

## Delivery notes
- **Pauses** (one beat each): after "Co ci wisi nad głową?"; after "never come back at all"; after "It does nothing without your tap."
- **Words to stress:** *exact* words · *one* thing · *your* tap · Gotowe.
- **Tone:** warm and dry, one joke at most, never needy. Say "it's early" plainly, then ask for help.
- **Never say:** "companion", "users", "growing", "does everything", or any number you can't show.
