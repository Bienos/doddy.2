# Doddy pitch

Every line here is true as of 17 October 2026. Re-check the bracketed items before each use:
- "Siri doesn't speak Polish" was true as of June 2026; re-check it.
- Never add user numbers, retention figures or a waitlist count until they exist in writing.

## The 1-minute pitch (base version, about 60 seconds)

> Quick question. Where's the parcel locker code your neighbour sent you last week? Notes? WhatsApp? Somewhere in four hundred screenshots?
>
> We all save little things everywhere, then lose them exactly when we need them. And chatbots sound very sure of themselves, even when they're guessing.
>
> So I built Doddy, a hand-drawn mate in your pocket. You tell him things by voice or text. Later, you just ask. He brings back your exact words, with when and where you saved them. And if he can't find it, he says so. No guessing. He never does anything without your tap.
>
> Siri still doesn't speak Polish. Doddy does, and English too.
>
> It's early. Doddy works on my iPhone, voice included, and the waitlist is open at doddy.lol. Now I need real people to break him.
>
> Join the beta. Tell Doddy before you forget.

*About 145 words.*

## Two openings

- **Funny, in Doddy's voice:** "Hi, I'm Doddy. I remember things, so you can keep forgetting them with confidence."
- **Serious:** "The things we lose most often aren't big. They're a code, a name, a book someone recommended."

## Three tailored versions (only the hook and the ask change)

### (a) Investor or accelerator
- **Hook:** "Every big AI company is building an agent that does everything. Hardly anyone trusts one with the small stuff yet. Doddy starts there: remembering exactly what you said, and proving it."
- **Ask:** "I'm not raising today. I'm running a 90-day beta to prove people come back to find what they saved. Join it, and if the numbers clear the bar I've set, I'd like 20 minutes with you in January."

### (b) Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs host or programme
- **Hook:** "I spent four years on the commercial side of tech, then learned to build with AI agents. Doddy is what I built."
- **Ask:** "I'm looking for a host in Spain or Portugal who has grown a consumer app, to learn how to turn beta testers into people who stay. And I'd love you to be one of my first testers too."

### (c) A potential early user
- **Hook:** "Do you have a WhatsApp chat with yourself? Thought so."
- **Ask:** "Join the beta at doddy.lol. Use him for two weeks, then give me 15 minutes to tell me what annoyed you."

## The 15-second version

> "Doddy is a hand-drawn mate in your iPhone. Tell him things before you forget. Later, he finds your exact words and shows where they came from, or admits he can't. He speaks Polish, which Siri still doesn't. Join the beta at doddy.lol."

## Delivery notes

**Pauses** (count one beat each):
- After "Somewhere in four hundred screenshots?" Let them check their own phone in their head.
- After "even when they're guessing."
- After "And if he can't find it, he says so." This is the line that sticks.

**Words to stress:** *exact* words · he *says so* · *your* tap · *real* people.

**Live demo** (about 15 seconds, during the solution part):
1. Before going on stage, save "The locker code is 4471".
2. On stage, ask out loud: "Doddy, what's the locker code?" Hold the phone up to show the card with the original text, the date and "Your note".
3. Ask something you never saved: "What's Marta's birthday?" Doddy says he couldn't find it. That refusal is the demo. It proves the honesty claim in two seconds.

**Backup:** voice sessions sometimes start twice or go silent (known bug). Keep a 20-second screen recording of the same demo ready. If voice hesitates for more than 3 seconds, type the question instead and say "voice is in testing", which is true and on brand.

**Tone:** warm and dry, one joke at most, and never needy. Don't apologise for being early. Say it plainly, then ask for help.

**Never say:** "users", "growing", "AI companion that does everything", or any number you can't show.
