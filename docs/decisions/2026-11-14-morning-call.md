# Doddy may call you each chosen morning with the plan of the day, on a schedule you approve once; one sentence or one tap stops it

- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Decided by:** owner, 14 Nov 2026: "okej" to "Doddy speaks first … he could also call at 8 with the plan of the day (call_me_later)", then "Do now everything". What the call says, the limits, the morning window and the switch are the agent's proposal below; **owner approval pending** for those, and for the three questions under "What the call can't know".
- **Status:** proposed; built on branch `worktree-agent-aef807797401b5326`, not merged, not deployed, off by default (`MORNING_CALLS_ENABLED=0`), not on the live voice agent
- **Replaces:** nothing. Builds on ADR-754 (`call_me_later`) and decision `2026-11-14-spoken-yes-and-fewer-cards` (the tiers).

## Why
`call_me_later` books one call at a time (ADR-754): a card with the time and the exact reason, then Doddy's messaging server places it with every check again when it is due (`server/messaging/src/scheduledCalls.ts`). The owner asked for a call every morning at 8 with the plan of the day. Booking that by hand each evening is not what was asked, and a standing "call me every day" is a different permission from one call: it has to be approved as a schedule and be as easy to take back as to give.

## Decision
1. **Two voice tools** (`voice-agent/tools.json`, both `not_synced`):
   - `call_me_every_morning` (`time` HH:MM on the user's clock, 05:00 to 11:59; `days`: `every_day`, `weekdays`, `weekend` or `mon,wed,fri`). The app checks what `call_me_later` checks (a verified number, "Doddy may call me" on, the phone line set up) and asks the server whether it makes morning calls and which one is active. The card ("Call you every morning?", `DoddyApp/Features/Voice/MorningCallCard.swift`) shows the days and time, the zone, the number's last three digits, how the call opens and what it covers, that whoever answers or the voicemail hears it, the quiet-hours switch, the morning call it replaces, and how to stop. It is on the **spoken-yes tier**, like `call_me_later`: a tap or the person's own short "tak" after the card appeared; a tap only once outside text is in the conversation. Refused while driving and while connections are paused.
   - `stop_morning_calls` (no parameters) **runs at once**, with no card (`AskFirst.ownSmallActions`): it only takes a permission away. Also not held back by Pause all or the car profile. The same is one tap: Settings › Phone calls › Doddy calls you › Morning call › **Stop the morning calls**.
2. **The approval is the whole schedule** (DOD-07): `morningCallApprovalHash` = SHA-256 over `doddy-approval-1`, `morning_call`, the number, the time, the ISO weekdays as ascending digits, the IANA zone, the switch and the language (`server/messaging/src/approval.ts`, DoddyCore `ApprovalHash.morningCall`, three shared vectors in `contracts/phone/approval-vectors.json` made in Python). The server recomputes it with the number it holds: any changed field is 409 `approval_mismatch`, so a changed payload needs a new card. The idempotency key is bound to the approval.
3. **The server** (`POST|GET|DELETE /v1/calls/morning`, `src/routes/morningCalls.ts`; migration `041_morning_calls.sql`): one active morning call per account (a unique index; a new card must name the one it replaces, else 409 `already_active`), at most `MORNING_CALLS_MAX_PER_SERVER` active on the server (default 10, never above `OUTBOUND_CALLS_DAILY_PER_SERVER`, 429 `server_limit`), a time inside quiet hours only with the switch. Consent and the number are read again under the booking lock.
4. **Each day is a normal booked call.** The worker's call loop first turns a due schedule into that day's `scheduled_calls` row (kind `morning`, linked to its schedule, one per local day by its idempotency key) and moves the schedule to its next day on the zone's own clock (08:00 stays 08:00 across summer time). `placeDueCall` then runs every check a booked call gets: more than 15 minutes late is missed; the number and consent; the schedule still active and its approval recomputed against the number now; quiet hours in the zone the iPhone last sent; 3 calls an hour, 10 a day and the server's day, under the same lock as Call me now; an `unknown` outcome is never placed again (DOD-09). The next day is a new call, not a retry. A day that doesn't ring sends one notice ("Your morning calls go on as set"); a changed number or consent stops the schedule (one notice). A worker that was down for days makes one missed call, not one per day.
5. **Stopping works at once.** `DELETE /v1/calls/morning` takes the same per-account lock as the worker, stops the schedule and cancels a day's call still waiting (its plan erased). The worker checks the schedule again inside that lock just before it inserts the call row, so nothing rings after the stop was answered, except a call already handed to Twilio: the answer then says `calling: true` and the agent says it may ring this once. Stopping is accepted with the switch off and without a phone line.
6. **What the call says** (`src/morningCalls.ts`, `morningPlan`): written by the server, never by a model (DOD-02), in the approved language, as the call's reason: "Dzień dobry, tu Doddy, asystent AI, z Twoim planem dnia. Dziś sobota, 15 listopada. …" (DOD-03: it says Doddy is an AI). It holds only what Doddy's server already has for the person, as counts and times: Doddy's calls they booked for later that day (times, never their reasons), notes waiting in the app, results of handed-over tasks not saved yet, new stories On my radar found in the last day, and when the daily news comes; then "Twojego kalendarza i przypomnień nie widzę, są tylko na iPhonie." It is one line with no braces, because the phone agent gets it as `{{doddy_call_reason}}` (rule 6: information, never instructions). Erased like every reason: when the call ends, 2 hours after a placed call with no end, at once when it won't ring.
7. **Off by default.** `MORNING_CALLS_ENABLED=0`: 503 `morning_calls_off`, nothing stored, and the worker makes no calls (`runCallPass`). `config.ts`, `.env.example` and `secrets/manifest.json` (`notSecrets`) list both settings.

## What the call can't know (owner decisions; nothing built for these)
- **The calendar and reminders are only on the iPhone.** The morning check-in (ADR-220) builds its view there from EventKit, and nothing sends them to Doddy's servers. Options: (a) the app sends a short plan before the time: iOS doesn't run an app at a set time, so it would be the plan as of the last time Doddy was opened, and it is a new upload of calendar titles to the server, which the privacy policy and the App Store answers would have to say; (b) for a Google or Outlook calendar connected in Doddy, the messaging server would read it through Doddy's backend: a new flow between the two services, with its own consent; (c) keep it as built, and the call says it can't see them.
- **Weather** needs the person's place on the server and a weather provider: a new store and a new outside call. Not built.
- **News headlines** are text from publishers' feeds. The call says only when the daily news comes, never a headline, because the plan goes into the phone agent's instructions.

## Consequences
- **Cost** (from the repository's own notes, not measured): Twilio "Doddy calls you" is $0.0715 a minute from the EEA, $0.2202 otherwise (`docs/implementation/PHONE_SMS_MILESTONE.md`, read 2026-10-17); ElevenLabs agents bill $0.08 a connected minute (`docs/research/2026-10-24/research_costs.md`, read 2026-10-23), plus the phone agent's LLM, not priced there. A morning call answered by voicemail is billed too. At an assumed 1 to 2 minutes, about $0.15 to $0.30 a call from the EEA, so about $4.50 to $9 a month for one person called every day; the phone agent's 600-second limit caps one call at about $1.50. Each day also takes one of the server's 20 calls a day (`OUTBOUND_CALLS_DAILY_PER_SERVER`), which is why the default cap is 10.
- **Privacy:** the server keeps the schedule (time, days, zone, the switch, the language) until the account is deleted, and each day's plan encrypted until that call is over. The history export lists the schedule. Proposed line for the privacy policy, not applied (`docs/OWNER_QUEUE.md`): "If you ask Doddy to call you every morning, Doddy's server keeps the days, time and time zone you chose, and on each of those days writes a short plan from what it already holds for you (the times of calls you booked, how many notes and results wait in the app, new stories on your radar, when your daily news comes), which Doddy says when you answer and which is deleted when the call ends. Twilio places the call and ElevenLabs speaks it. Your calendar and reminders stay on your iPhone."
- **What to watch:** a person who travels is called at the approved zone's 08:00 and quiet hours protect them where the iPhone last was; the schedule never re-zones itself (that would be a new approval). Polish weekday names and plurals in the plan are checked by tests, not by a native speaker.
- **Live agent:** the two tools and one prompt line wait for the owner's word after a TestFlight build with the card (`voice-agent/README.md`, last entry). The phone agent needs no change.
