# Doddy has one voice: the choice of voice and its samples are removed

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decided by:** owner ("tak, otwórz PR i usuń wybór głosu", 12 Nov 2026, after "jeżeli chodzi o głos, to nie wiem, czy powinna być opcja na przykład zmieniania głosu […] może to byśmy wyjebać"; the 9 Nov decision already quoted "usuń również opcję wyboru głosu")
- **Status:** accepted; built on branch `claude/peaceful-allen-1mh9uq`
- **Replaces:** the voice half of ADR-105 (six voices to choose from) and ADR-122 (bundled voice samples). Completes `2026-11-09-elevenlabs-only-voice`, which removed the provider picker but left the choice of ElevenLabs voice in Settings › Voice › Voice and personality › Voice.

## Why
The review of 12 Nov 2026 (`docs/strategy/PRZEGLAD_APLIKACJI_2026-11-12.md`, section 3) found the choice of six voices (Doddy, Josh, Wiktor, River, Jessica, Will) still in Settings, three screens deep, each titled "Voice", with a footer saying the voices were "candidates while Doddy's final voice is chosen". The owner wants Doddy to sound like one character.

## Decision
- **DoddyCore:** `VoiceChoice` is gone; `VoiceStyle` is speed and expressiveness only, and its override never carries a voice (`VoiceOverride.voiceID` stays in the type, always nil, so the voice session reads one shape). The profile export no longer has a `voice` field.
- **App:** Settings › Voice and personality keeps Speed, Expressiveness and "Starts in" on one section; the Voice list, the play buttons, "Hear …", "Use Doddy's own voice" and `VoicePreviewPlayer` are gone, and the six sample clips (`DoddyApp/Resources/VoicePreviews`, ~540 KB) are deleted. The Settings row shows the personality summary only.
- **A voice picked before:** no longer read or sent, so everyone hears the agent's own voice from the next conversation. The stored `voice.choice` value is left in UserDefaults unread (`PreferenceKey.voiceChoice` stays in `RootView.swift`, a file other pull requests have open).
- **Not changed:** the live ElevenLabs agent (its voice, and its permission to accept a voice override, which the app simply stops using); `AudioSessionPolicy.voicePreview`, now unused.

## Consequences
- One voice everywhere: the app, the island, phone calls. Fewer strings to translate (13 lines left every language table).
- Anyone who picked another voice hears Doddy's own voice again, without being told. Acceptable in a beta.
- The licence question on the six clips (DEPENDENCIES_AND_LICENSES.md, "Voice samples") goes away with the clips.

## Evidence
