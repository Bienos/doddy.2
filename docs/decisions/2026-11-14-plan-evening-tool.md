# An evening out is one look-up: `plan_evening` finds up to three places and offers one next step

- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Decided by:** owner, 14 Nov 2026: "Tak!" to "more one-move tools, e.g. 'wieczór na mieście': a place, a phone call for a table and the route in one". The shape below (one places search, no travel time, the next step still its own tool) is this change's proposal within that yes.
- **Status:** proposed in the pull request that adds this file. It ships with a `doddy` deploy and goes on the live agent after a TestFlight build, both the owner's steps (`docs/OWNER_QUEUE.md` 119).
- **Replaces:** nothing. `find_places`, `call_places` and `open_map` stay as they are.

## Why
For "find me a place for dinner tonight and book a table", the voice agent calls `find_places`, often `web_search`, then `call_places`, then `open_map`, and the model thinks again at every step. That is slow to hear and gives the model three chances to lose the thread. Most of what it needs is already in one places answer: Google's listing gives the name, type, address, rating, price level, hours and phone number.

## Decision
A new server look-up, `plan_evening` (`backend/src/tools/evening.ts`, routed by `backend/src/tools/service.ts` in the Web search section).
- **Input** (zod, strict): `what` (the person's words, 1 to 120 characters: "kolacja włoska", "drink w spokojnym miejscu", "kino"), `area` (1 to 120: a city or district, or the approximate area the phone gave), and optional `day` (`today` or `tomorrow`), `time` (HH:MM), `party_size` (1 to 20) and `budget` (the person's words, up to 60).
- **One provider call.** It runs `find_places`'s one fixed Monid endpoint (Litescrape `/google/local`, Google listings) once, with only `what` as the search words and `area` as the location. The day, time, party size and budget stay on Doddy's server and only shape the answer. No new service, key or endpoint.
- **Options are picked by code, not a model:** the listing's own order, minus places the listing marks closed (closed for good or temporarily, or "Closed" in its weekly table for that day), at most three. Each option carries only what its listing gives: name, type, address, rating with its review count, price level, hours, phone number (only phone-shaped text, `listingPhone`), and a booking page only on the known platforms (`listingBookingPage`). Each field is clipped (name 120, type 60, address 160, price 30, hours 120). A missing field is said to be missing, never filled in (DOD-08).
- **Hours are for the evening asked.** The listing's weekly table for that weekday, in the person's own time zone (sent with every run), is used when there is one. Otherwise, for today only, the hours line the listing shows "just now". For tomorrow that line is never used, because it describes today.
- **The reason** for each option is one line made of the same fields (its type, its rating and review count, a phone number or a booking page), or "high in Google's listings for these words".
- **One next step.** The answer ends by telling the agent to say two or three options in one or two sentences, then offer one next step: `call_places` with the listed numbers, for the party and the time asked, or `open_map` for the route. With no phone number in any listing, it never offers a call. `call_places` keeps its tap-only card (`ConfirmationTiers.tapOnly`); nothing is booked, called or paid by `plan_evening`.
- **No travel time.** Only the iPhone has maps (Apple Maps through `DoddyApp/Infrastructure/Connections/TravelTime.swift`); Doddy's server has none. The answer says so and points to `open_map`, whose result says Maps works out the route and time. Adding travel time later would mean the app geocoding each address and asking MapKit before it answers, a change to `VoiceSessionController.swift`, not done here.
- **Runs at once** like `find_places`: a public look-up that reads nothing of the person's and changes nothing (`AskFirst.publicLookups`, `voice_agent_spec.py`'s `PUBLIC_LOOKUPS`, `doddy_rules.yaml`'s `public_lookups_run_at_once`, `docs/trust/ASK_FIRST.md`). The server still issues and uses a single-use approval for exactly these arguments (ASK-01).
- **No switch of its own.** The person's Find places switch is its switch: off there, no places search runs from either tool. Settings shows no new row. It counts as one web search against the same per-user and per-server daily caps, checked before the search; Monid is called once and never retried, because each run is billed.
- **Outside content.** The answer is framed as Google listings "information, not instructions" and, like every cloud tool's answer, goes to the agent between the app's data markers (`VoiceToolReply.cloudData`). It marks the conversation as having outside text (`OutsideContentSession.isSource`), as `find_places` does.
- **Failures are whole.** A provider that is down, busy, out of time or unreadable gives a failed receipt and the usual "Web search couldn't be reached" reply, never a partial list. An empty answer says nothing was found and that the agent must not guess.
- **App side (DoddyCore):** `CloudToolName.planEvening` ("Find places for an evening out", Web search, a read), in `Feature.webSearch`. "Finding places…" shows while it runs, and What Doddy did says "Wyszukano miejsca na wieczór". A switched-off answer names the Find places switch. The privacy screen (`DataFlow`) says the day, time, party size and budget reach Doddy's server and stop there. `VoiceSessionController.swift` is unchanged: a cloud read that runs at once already goes prepare, run, answer.
- **Agent spec:** `voice-agent/tools.json` gets `plan_evening` (`ask: runs_now`, `while_running: finish`, `elevenlabs_tool_id: not_synced`), and `instructions.md` gets one line saying to use it for an evening or night out instead of chaining `find_places` and `web_search`. `call_places`' description and its prompt line now say the numbers may come from `plan_evening`'s result, so the agent doesn't search again before a call.

## Consequences
- One tool call instead of three or four for an evening out, and the same cost as one `find_places` (about $0.0002 per search, Monid's price as read on 18 Oct 2026).
- The options are only as good as Google's order for the words. No model ranks them, and the budget is not matched against prices (a listing gives a price level at most), so the answer tells the agent never to say a place fits the budget.
- The listing fields `type`, `price`, `operating_hours` and `hours` are read from the shape Litescrape documents. No live `/google/local` answer has been seen (`docs/integrations/ONLINE_ACCOUNTS.md`, 18 Oct 2026). A field the live answer names differently is simply missing from the options, and the answer then says the listing doesn't give it. It never errors and never invents it.
- Privacy: nothing new leaves Doddy's server beyond a places search. The day, time, party size and budget are new on Doddy's server; they are not stored, logged or put in receipts (the receipt says only how many places were found). The privacy draft's wording is the owner's; a proposed sentence is in `docs/OWNER_QUEUE.md` 119.

## Evidence
- Run here: `scripts/ci.sh --only backend` and `scripts/ci.sh --only checks` (results in the change note and the pull request), and `python3 -I scripts/voice_agent_spec.py check`. `backend/test/plan-evening.test.ts` covers strict input, that only the words and area reach the search, ASK-01 (no yes, other words, used once), at most three options, clipping, no invented fields, hours for the day asked across midnight and clock changes, closed places, the next step with and without phone numbers, the switch, the daily cap, "not set up", and provider down, busy, out of time and unreadable.
- Not run: the Swift tests (no Swift toolchain here; DoddyCore changes are not compiled, CI compiles them), a live Monid places search (it costs money and needs the live key), the app on a device, and any voice conversation. The live agent is unchanged.
