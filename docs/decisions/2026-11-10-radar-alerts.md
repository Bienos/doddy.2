# On my radar: matching that works in Polish, nicer adding, own feeds, and alerts the person lets Doddy's server check

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Decided by:** owner, 10 Nov 2026 (in Polish, translated): "I added a cryptocurrency section and it doesn't work. Adding needs to be nicer. Research how Doddy can track information and sometimes send notifications about news or mini summaries." Later: "The user should be able to set it up by voice … or in settings. Simple, nice, automated, showing the connected sources; someone might connect their own RSS feed; say when Doddy should tell them about an event, by keywords … Is that even possible?" The shape below (opt-in per follow, flag off by default) is this pull request's proposal; **owner approval pending** for turning the flag on.
- **Status:** proposed (built behind `RADAR_ALERTS_ENABLED`, off)
- **Replaces:** nothing. Extends ADR-656 (Following) and ADR-657 (`follow_news`); reuses ADR-174/175 (daily news) and the outbox.

## Why

Read on 10 Nov 2026 (read only):

- The live messaging server logs `newsSearch: false` at every start (Render log, 09:11 to 16:51 UTC): `GNEWS_API_KEY` is not set. A follow of the person's own words was therefore looked for **only inside the stories of their other follows** (by default the four Poland feeds), with a plain substring test (`rank.ts`), so "kryptowaluty" never found "kryptowalut", "kryptowalucie" or "Bitcoina". A words follow such as "Krypto" showed nothing, or almost nothing.
- The catalog's crypto topic had **one** publisher, CoinDesk, at an address that answers 308 (a redirect to the same path without the slash). From this sandbox the redirect is followed and the feed parses (25 stories, all within two days); whether Render's address gets the same answer is not known (the database's cache rows can't be read from here: its IP allowlist is empty).
- Tapping a follow's circle did nothing, so a topic's stories were mixed into 24 Poland cards and could be cut by the 40-card limit; nothing said which publishers a follow reads or that a publisher didn't answer.
- The owner's 12:27 UTC session shows six feed requests within 20 seconds after the catalog was loaded (the add sheet), all 200, consistent with "added, saw nothing, pulled again".

## Decision

1. **Matching (server `news/match.ts`, DoddyCore `RadarMatch`, one shared fixture `schemas/fixtures/radar_match_v1.json`, 31 cases):** words are folded (case, diacritics, ł) and compared by their shared beginning: up to 4 letters the whole word ("CPK", "AI" never inside "rain"), longer words may change their last 1 to 3 letters depending on length and on whether they end in a vowel ("Tesla"→"Tesli", "kryptowaluty"→"kryptowalucie", "Orlen"→"Orlenu", but "Polska" never "Polsat" and "sport" never "sporo"). Multi-word keywords must appear in order ("Elon Musk"→"Elona Muska"). Not a stemmer; predictable and tested.
2. **Where words are looked for (feed only):** in every catalog feed (33 feeds, shared 30-minute cache) and the person's own feeds, not only their other follows. The daily digest keeps its old pool.
3. **Crypto:** CoinDesk at its canonical address, plus Cointelegraph, Decrypt, The Block and Bitcoin.pl (Polish), each fetched and parsed here on 10 Nov 2026 (20 to 38 dated stories each). Europe gains BBC News Europe (The Guardian's Europe feed answered with an HTML page from here). Every catalog feed has a short publisher name, used on cards and as the follow's sources ("CoinDesk", not "CoinDesk: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Crypto News and Price Data").
4. **The feed answer** says per follow which publishers it read and whether each answered (`follows[].sources`), or how many feeds were searched for words (`searchedFeeds`), and every follow gets its best 4 stories before a busy topic fills the 40.
5. **The screen:** a tap on a follow shows only its stories with its sources, its "tell me when" line and an honest "nothing in the last two days" line. One **Add to radar** sheet: type anything; Doddy offers the catalog topic it points at (hints in Polish and English, e.g. "bitcoin"→Kryptowaluty with its publishers), the words themselves, or, for an address, the person's own RSS/Atom feed (checked first through `POST /v1/news/feeds/check`, https only, then `safeFetch`'s public-address, redirect, size and time limits on every fetch).
6. **Alerts, opt-in per follow:** "Tell me when a story mentions …" words and how: **Here only** (default; nothing leaves the iPhone but the feed request), **As it happens**, or **Once a day** at a chosen time. The last two need the separate switch **"Let Doddy's server check this for you"**, whose footer says what the server keeps; without it nothing is sent. `PUT /v1/news/radar` replaces the account's list with exactly the follows that have the switch on (an empty list is always accepted, even with the flag off). The app says a check is on only when the server's answer lists it.
7. **Server (migration `026_radar_alerts.sql`, new tables only):** `radar_alerts` (the follow, words, mode, time, time zone, language, `watch_from`) and `radar_hits` (one row per account and story ever, which is also the dedupe; 14 days). A worker loop (`radarLoop`, every 30 s; each instant alert about every 20 minutes, daily ones every 2 hours and at their time) claims due alerts with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and moves their next check first, reads the follow's feeds through the shared cache, records new matching stories published after the alert was turned on, and queues **at most one push per alert per pass** through `outbox.enqueue` (`radar:<alert>:<hit>` idempotency key, encrypted at rest):
   - as it happens: `On your radar · Tesla: “<the publisher's headline>” (Reuters)` / `Na radarze · …: „…” (…)`, `+N more in Doddy`; held in quiet hours (21:00–08:00 in the alert's time zone, `QUIET_HOURS_*`); a story found more than 6 hours after it was published is listed in the app only; at most 8 radar pushes per account in 24 hours, the rest in the app only;
   - once a day: a count and up to three lines with each publisher named; lines by the existing news writer only when the server has one, through `validateLines` (a line for a story not in the list is dropped), with "Lines written by AI from the publishers' headlines." added; otherwise the headlines word for word; a summary more than 2 hours late is not sent.
   - No notification possible (no device token, no push sender, production without the encryption key): the story is recorded as shown in the app. `GET /v1/news/radar` lists what was found (with `push: false`), the screen shows it under **Doddy spotted** with a dot on the follow, and `POST /v1/news/radar/seen` clears the dot.
8. **Switch:** `RADAR_ALERTS_ENABLED` (config, `render.yaml` for both services, `.env.example`), default `"0"`: new alerts answer 503 `radar_disabled`, no worker loop runs.
9. **Voice:** unchanged in this pull request (`follow_news` already adds a follow by voice). Proposed next step: optional `alert_words` and `tell_me` arguments, kept on the iPhone as "Here only" until the person turns on the server switch on screen (a server check stays a tap, never a voice yes). Not built: it needs `voice-agent/tools.json` and `VoiceSessionController.swift`, which another session is editing.

## Consequences

- **Privacy:** a follow moves to the server only behind its own switch; "What leaves this iPhone" (`DataFlow.swift`) and the privacy policy draft (section 5.15) say what is kept and for how long. Deleting the account or turning the switch off deletes it (cascade; tested). Logs carry counts and SQLSTATE codes only (tested with planted words).
- **Constitution:** feed text is data, quoted and attributed, never instructions (R-TOOLS); no AI writes an instant alert; a daily summary's AI lines are checked against real stories and labelled; "checked by Doddy's server" is shown only from the server's own answer (no claim without a verified write).
- **Cost:** the first word-follow request after a cache expiry fetches up to 33 feeds (shared cache, 30 minutes). Each alert check is one cached gather. Pushes: at most 8 per account per day.
- **Free Render plan:** the worker runs inside the web service, which sleeps when idle, so checks only happen while it is awake; a reliable radar needs an always-on instance or the separate worker service (owner decision).
- **APNs:** pushes only reach a phone once APNs works in production (`docs/OWNER_QUEUE.md` item 8); until then everything is in the app list, and the sheet says so.
- **The privacy policy page on the site is not changed**; the draft is, and a line must be added before the switch goes on.
