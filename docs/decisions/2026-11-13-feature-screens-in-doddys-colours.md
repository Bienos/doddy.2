# Home's feature screens start with one question in Doddy's colours, and keep only what is theirs

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** owner, in a voice note about each screen: Trips ("to pole jest bez sensu … znajdź moje w Gmailu też jest bez sensu … skupić się na zaplanowaniu wyjazdu, na wyszukiwaniu lotów, hoteli … w kolorach Dodiego"), On my radar ("design poprawiony … z jakich źródeł można podłączyć … podsumowanie artykułu AI-owego, z modelem Haiku"), Poland Pack ("nie wiem, o co tu chodzi … rzeczy się powtarzają … InPost jest w ustawieniach"), Food ("pole do zamawiania … chcę burgera i to mi pokazuje … z tych wszystkich apek"), Shopping ("najtaniej w tym tygodniu … trzeba złączyć", "jedzeniowe oddzielnie, ciuchowe i inne oddzielnie", "wyszukujesz, porównujesz … ustawienia cen, szukania okazji"), Today ("zero edycji, nie można nic zrobić").
- **Status:** proposed in this branch
- **Replaces:** the screen layouts of decisions `2026-11-11-travel-agent-plan` (Trips), `2026-11-11-food-button` (Food) and the Poland Pack screen (#333); the "Cheapest this week" row on Home's menu.

## Decision
- **One look.** `DoddyApp/DesignSystem/DoddyScreenKit.swift`: an orange hero (`DoddyHero`, the brand's `#FF6A00` as a surface, ink text on it, a pose from the owner's pack), a cream field with a go button, chips, cream cards, tags and ink/outlined buttons, all from `Theme`. Used by Trips, On my radar, My city, Food and the Shopify screen.
- **Trips:** "Where are we flying?" with Flights and Stays. Flights: the planner's conversation inline and each option as a boarding pass (codes, times, length, stops, the real total, traps, Book on Kiwi.com, stays). Stays: a place, check-in, nights and people build Booking.com and Airbnb searches on the iPhone (`StaySearch` in DoddyCore) and can be handed to Doddy's background research with its yes card. "Find my trips in Gmail" left the screen: it is "Import bookings from Gmail" in the ••• menu, and its card shows what leaves the iPhone before anything runs. `TripPlannerView` is gone; saved trips are cards under "Your trips".
- **On my radar:** an orange hero with Add and Sources. Sources (`RadarSourcesSheet`) lists every topic the server reads with its publishers, followed with one tap, and explains own words and own feeds. Each story has "AI summary": `StorySummarySheet` asks `POST /v1/news/summary` (Claude Haiku on the messaging server, off until `NEWS_SUMMARY_ENABLED=1`), labels the result as AI with the model and the page it came from, and never shows a partial answer. Cards themselves stay the publishers' own words.
- **My city** (was "Polska na co dzień" / Poland Pack): the weather now as the hero, air and warnings as tiles, warning details, NBP rates with a converter. The leaflets row (it is Shopping › Groceries) and the InPost section (Settings › Parcels) left.
- **Food:** "What do you feel like?" with a field and quick picks; every delivery app then offers to look for exactly that: a tap copies the words (this iPhone only, two minutes) and opens the app, where the person pastes them. Doddy reads no menus or prices from the apps, so it never says which is nearest or cheapest, and orders nothing.
- **Shopping:** the screen is the one from `2026-11-13-shopping-one-field` (merged from `main` as #407 and #423): one field, "What do you need?", that code routes to the leaflets, to "Find me this" or to both. An earlier version of this branch had its own redesign of the same screen (a Groceries / Everything else switcher with categories); the two couldn't be merged without a compiler, and `main`'s was already merged and came from the owner's review of 12 Nov, so `main`'s wins and this branch's was dropped. "Cheapest this week" keeps its row on Home's menu (`main`'s UI tests open it there). What stays from this branch: the **Shopify screen** (`ShopifyLiveShoppingView`) compares a product's offers side by side, cheapest first and marked, with the price spread, sorting and an in-stock filter.
- **Today:** a tap on a reminder opens a sheet to change its title and day or time, tick it off or delete it; Add makes a new one. Every write goes to Reminders on this iPhone and is read back before the card says it happened (`ReminderEdit` in DoddyCore, `TodayModel.edit`, `.delete`, `.add`). A due is moved or added here, never cleared.

## Consequences
- Not done, and why: ordering food for the person (needs a wallet or card, owner queue 73); prices across delivery apps (no public data); more shops in Shopping (each needs an API or partner account, owner queue 74); hotel prices inside the app (needs a stays API, owner queue 75). None is shown on screen as if it worked.
- The radar's AI summary sends a story's link and headline to Doddy's server and the page's text to Anthropic; the privacy policy must say so before it is switched on (owner queue 76).
- The Groceries / Everything else switcher and the shopping categories (electronics, home and garden, fashion, other, each with its own "what matters most") are not in the app. They could come back on top of `main`'s one field as a follow-up if the owner still wants them: ask.
