## Evidence and limits

- **INFERRED** means a consequence of the inspected code that still needs an integration/device experiment.
- **NOT YET VERIFIED** means access or execution evidence is missing. Historical deployment statements in `STATUS.md` and `voice-agent/README.md` remain historical records.

## What the user's observations establish

Excess confirmation has concrete code/prompt causes for handoff and inquiry calling. The stronger claim that DODDY cannot do background work is contradicted by the existing server implementation. Background visibility and report evidence handling have concrete gaps. Proactive behavior is feature-specific, not a general capability. Whether the live voice agent unnecessarily asks before each lookup, calls fail in production, or real reports omit the most important facts still needs the actual agent config, installed build, task/call traces and appropriate historical-call access.

The proposed division of responsibilities is largely already present: ElevenLabs handles realtime voice; typed phone/server code owns authority; workers own long-running work. The improvement is to connect and instrument those pieces, not hand authority or persistent execution to another prompt.
