# Doddy: current status (read this first)

Doddy is a voice-first companion for iPhone and iPad. The Apple Watch app and the Mac menu bar prototype are **paused** (ADR-572). This page says what is true now, in one page. **Replace facts when they change; don't add dated log paragraphs.** What a pull request changed goes in `changes/` (see `changes/README.md`); the full log up to 7 Nov 2026 is in `docs/archive/STATUS-log-2026-10-08-to-2026-11-07.md`.

## What changed in the product since 7 Nov (from `main`, not on a device)
- **Doddy account required** (`2026-11-08-required-account`). The welcome signs in with **Apple or Google**; email codes are hidden (`2026-11-09-email-sign-in-off-for-now`). Doddy Web: Apple or Google opens the same account as the iPhone, and on a computer it opens in a small window (#374). Your Doddy account is under Settings › About.
- **Every feature in every build** (`2026-11-10-no-beta-scope`): the beta list is gone, the hide mechanism stays tested.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs only (`2026-11-09-elevenlabs-only-voice`) and one voice, no picker (`2026-11-12-one-voice`). What you ask for runs at once; changes, sends, deletes, calls and paid work keep their card (`2026-11-11-asked-for-runs-at-once`). Cards pop up from the bottom and the newest ask wins; finished tasks and files arrive in Messages (`2026-11-10-cards-pop-up-and-results-in-messages`). The working line names the tool and says "Still on it…" (`2026-11-12-voice-turn-feedback`). Faster start: a bounded memory snapshot, a token fetched ahead, network preparation (no speed-up measured). Focus and wind-down by voice; "Doddy looks at the screen" (one shared picture, `2026-11-09-doddy-looks-at-the-screen`); the translator starts in silence; no "AI" label in the app's own words (`2026-11-09-companion-not-ai-label`; the voice still says he is an AI when asked).
- **Action Button and island:** the island comes up first and the connection follows (`2026-11-10-action-button-start`); Doddy keeps his own mixable audio session in the background so music keeps playing; the island keeps one width and opens big only if "Expand when Doddy speaks" is on (`2026-11-12-action-button-audio-and-island`); Home's fine lines are back (`2026-11-10-island-fine-lines-back`).
- **Home and screens:** Home ••• has What I can do, Phone calls, On my radar, Food, Shopping and Trips. Today at a glance (`2026-11-10-today-at-a-glance`), Tasks redesigned with typed hand-over (`2026-11-10-tasks-redesign`), Settings with one Voice and personality page and a Phone calls hub (`2026-11-12-settings-one-voice-page`, `2026-11-12-phone-calls-hub`), HomeKit says at once when a build can't use it.
- **Messages and files:** save or find notes, lists and reminders from a card (`2026-11-09-messages-local-actions`); finished tasks arrive while the app is open and the push wakes it (`2026-11-12-results-reach-messages`); a file shows a dot until ready and fails honestly; a bare request for a file asks for details first (`2026-11-10-files-ask-for-details`); decks are researched and designed (`2026-11-10-better-presentations`), and retry once when Anthropic refuses the outline's schema (#327). **No deck has come out of the live pipeline yet** (11 Nov).
- **Shopping:** one Shopping screen ("Find me this", searches, watched prices, leaflets); "Check this" for a pasted or shared product link (price watches need `WATCHES_ENABLED`, set on 12 Nov); Shopify shops' products with a cart prepared at the shop, never paid by Doddy (#369); "Cheapest this week" and a leaflet catalog read once for everyone (`2026-11-12-leaflet-catalog`). A live test read prices from 42% of 80 shop pages; x-kom, Media Expert, Euro RTV AGD, Allegro and Decathlon block it.
- **Calls and texts:** restaurant calls (`call_places`, up to three places), call-backs, `call_me_later`, `call_me_now`, `text_me`; calls that book go to a separate booking agent that may accept only the booking on the card (`2026-11-12-book-agent`; the app card is merged, `call_places` is not on the live agent yet, owner queue 48). Salon booking pages are offered, never booked. **No real call or text has been seen end to end.**
- **Flights and travel:** Kiwi.com search by voice is on (`FLIGHT_SEARCH_ENABLED=1`); Duffel test bookings are off (`2026-11-11-flights-kiwi-search-duffel-booking`). Trips from booking emails in Gmail and a trip planner (`2026-11-11-travel-agent-plan`; nothing is booked).
- **Food:** a Food button and `open_food_app` by voice (opens the app or the restaurant's own page after a tap; not on the live agent, owner queue 46). Uber Direct is server-only and off (`2026-11-12-uber-direct-deliveries`). Food by phone is a design (`2026-11-12-food-by-phone`).
- **On my radar:** Polish-aware matching, crypto from five publishers, an Add sheet, alerts set by voice (`2026-11-10-radar-alerts`, `2026-11-11-radar-alerts-by-voice`); alerts are off on the server.
- **Conversation recaps:** all five steps are merged (`2026-11-09-conversation-recaps`), both switches off by default; the server side is deployed.
- **Logbook and admin:** the iPhone counts usage (never words), on by default; `/admin` once the owner sets it up (`2026-11-09-usage-counts-on-by-default`).
- **Other:** Poland Pack v1 (IMGW, GIOŚ, NBP, InPost; #333), BLIK transfer drafts are back, every build shows its commit in Settings › About.

## Known problems
- **`main` fails its whole UI suite** since 12 Nov (see Code). Nobody has looked at which test.
- **Security review, 12 Nov 2026** (read-only, not a penetration test; an artifact in the owner's account): old Supabase functions without sign-in still run in the accounts project, one of them an open OpenAI proxy; the old Render copies above still run; a sign-out leaves the last person's data for the next one on a shared device; deleting an account can report success while data stays on the messaging server, and ElevenLabs keeps conversations; about four accounts can use up the shared hourly voice limit. Fixes are offered as separate pull requests; the console steps are the owner's.
- **Google sign-in fails** with `redirect_uri_mismatch` until Supabase's callback is registered on the Google OAuth client (owner queue 25). Apple on the website needs owner queue 28.
- **No dollar spend limits** on Anthropic, OpenAI or Monid (owner queue 2, 13). The Anthropic key was replaced on 9 Nov; a deck request on 11 Nov got past it (its outline failed with a 400, not a 401), but no success is recorded (owner queue 31).
- **No staging environment:** Debug, TestFlight and production builds all use the production servers and voice agent. The plan is Doddy Beta on staging (ADR-812).
- **The privacy policy is out of date:** it says the app has no analytics and misses Logbook, Messages on by default, recaps, the screen look, radar alerts, decks' Wikipedia reads, runs-at-once reads, Uber and flight passenger data (owner queue 7, 24, 26, 31 to 34, 37, 42, 43). The camera consent text says a photo never leaves the phone, but one sent with a question goes to Claude.
- **The hosted Mac is a queue:** `ios-build` waits 45 minutes or more when several pull requests run (owner queue 44). Its simulator sometimes freezes during the native island test, which runs alone and last under a watchdog (ADR-764).
- **`doddy` runs with `env: development`** (its own start log, 12 Nov), so production-only safeguards are off; the security review lists it. Setting the environment is the owner's step on Render.
- **A client asks `PUT /v1/web/logbook/state` about every 10 seconds and gets `503 live_not_configured`** (`doddy`'s log, 12 Nov 15:07 to 15:08 UTC). Which client, and which setting is missing, is not known yet.
- **Doddy Web's browser test for a deck download fails** (it expects the old demo text; the backend now returns a real .pptx). These tests run only by hand (`BUILD_AND_TEST.md`).
- **Silent voice sessions** were seen in October (`docs/implementation/VOICE_SESSION_STARTS.md`). Confirming the cause still needs a device log.
- **`docs/OWNER_QUEUE.md` has two rows numbered 31 and two numbered 34**; cite them by their words as well as the number.
- **Not run in this review (12 Nov):** Swift tests and the app build (no Swift or Xcode in the session), the messaging database tests, anything on a device, and the live voice agent, Supabase, Twilio and App Store Connect.
