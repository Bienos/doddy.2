## 1. Executive verdict

### Decision in one sentence

**[I] Develop Doddy's character as a potentially valuable media/distribution asset, while testing a single recurring, measurable assistant job with the existing prototype; invest in a rebuilt app only after user-observed benefit and safe, plausible delivery are established.**

### What this means in practice

1. **Keep both assets, separate their scorecards.** Entertainment is measured in qualified audience response; software in completed jobs, comparative advantage and natural repeated usage. One cannot validate the other. [R: D01 Evaluation §5; D05 Evaluation §§4–6]
2. **Investigate follow-through first, not because it has won, but because success and failure can be observed.** Compare it openly with personal-context retrieval and live translation. [I]
3. **Use the current prototype as a learning instrument, not as presumed production infrastructure.** Perform a read-only inventory and narrowly repair only what is needed for ethical tests. [R: both evaluations]
4. **Do not compete on “voice + memory + personality” alone.** Those are already offered in overlapping form by ChatGPT, Character.AI, Apple and other products, subject to device/plan variations. [V: EX04–EX07]
5. **Keep launch claims and monetisation restrained.** No target cohort, switching advantage, retained-user CAC, willingness to pay, subscription price, revenue forecast, or legal clearance has been verified. [R]

**Highest-impact unknown:** Is there an *adult Polish iPhone user with a naturally recurring, presently unsatisfied job* for whom Doddy performs sufficiently better than their **real best substitute** that they choose it again without being prompted? Everything else depends on that answer.

### Why this is the decision, not a rejection

- **For continuing:** broad GenAI familiarity exists; real everyday assistant jobs are plausible; a character can be tested inexpensively; a working prototype may reduce testing cost. [V: EX01–EX03; R: D01 Research §§3–4]
- **Against scaling:** 64% of EU GenAI non-users in a Eurostat survey reported **no perceived need**; existing tools already solve many use cases; AI subscriber retention in RevenueCat's selected sample is relatively weak; no Doddy cohort has been observed. [V: EX01, EX04, EX08]
- **Against delaying all character production:** independent entertainment/IP value is possible even if the app hypothesis fails. That upside does **not** license an unvalidated general-purpose app. [I]

## 3. Strategy choices and decision matrix

**Scoring convention:** **++** favourable; **+** somewhat favourable; **0** uncertain/mixed; **−** unfavourable; **?** insufficient evidence. These are **reasoned comparative assessments**, not empirical market scores. Reversibility means the ability to abandon after a short experiment without significant sunk software cost.

| Option | Recurring-use path | Differentiation | Solo-founder feasibility | Distribution advantage | Subscription evidence | Reversibility | Recommended role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Character-first media** | ? | + (brand identity) | + if production templates are cheap | + possible; **unmeasured** | ? | **High** | Bounded parallel audience experiment |
| **B. Utility-first general assistant** | 0 | − against incumbents | − | ? | ? | **Low** after broad build | **Reject for now** |
| **C. Two-track character + selected job** | + if job real | + possible | **0** capacity split | + optional | ? | **High if capped** | **Primary strategy** |
| **D. Companionship-first relationship app** | ? | 0; existing character voice | 0 / − safety burden | + possible | ? | Medium | Separate speculative venture; not default |
| **E. Focused one-job assistant, expressive UI** | **+ testable** | 0 until baseline beaten | **++** bounded scope | 0 | ? | **High** | **Primary product method** within C |
| **F. Pure character IP (no app)** | Not a utility job | + possible | + | + possible | Media revenue untested | **High** | Pivot option if software fails |

**Recommended combination:** **C as the company-level approach; E as the software-development discipline; A as a capped distribution/IP experiment.** [I]

**Why not others:** B duplicates an extensive commodity stack without evidence of a reason to switch. D may create engagement, but currently lacks a measured healthy outcome, has serious boundary/privacy concerns and faces incumbents. F abandons software upside prematurely, but remains a legitimate outcome after poor app evidence.

**Strongest counterarguments:**

1. A broad long-term memory relationship might become valuable only after months; a narrow task can underestimate network/personalisation effects. **Response:** use longitudinal tests after identifying consented repeat use; a broad data architecture is not the first experiment.
2. Entertainment might be the product, not a distribution channel. **Response:** then underwrite it as a distinct entertainment business with separate welfare, production and revenue economics.
3. A solo founder may build a narrow feature faster than scheduling dozens of interviews. **Response:** build small *test instruments* where cheaper; do not confuse shipping a demo with proving demand.
4. A charismatic character can make commodity tools feel dramatically better. **Response:** plausible but directly testable by holding task, model, voice and access constant and changing presentation.

### Recommended first experiment: “Say it once. Follow through.”

**[H]** When interrupted or away from a keyboard, a user may want to say “Doddy, remind me to send the quote to Anna tomorrow at 10” or “Remember where I left the car,” then receive correct confirmation and later retrieve/complete the task. This is a *test script*, **not proof that an integration exists**. Avoid personal names in telemetry. Compare with native Reminders/Siri under identical conditions.

**Minimal credible experience:** launch by ordinary user-accessible means → capture spoken/text intent → clarify ambiguous date/recipient → user confirms → save to a permitted, clearly described destination → show acknowledgement + later retrievable status → allow correction/deletion → report errors transparently. If the relevant native write cannot be implemented safely, use a labelled **manual/concierge** step and never represent it as autonomous execution.

**Explicit outcome:** verified, correctly saved/handled intention (not a pleasing AI reply), plus time/steps/effort and voluntary next-episode reuse. If Doddy cannot beat the current best method on a meaningful dimension, **reject this wedge**.

## 6. Doddy the Character — lean validation design

### Character hypothesis

**[H]** A consistent expressive, friendly, slightly cheeky Doddy improves initial understanding, recall and willingness to engage with useful workflows while supporting independently worthwhile entertainment. **Never optimise for dependency or imply Doddy is human.**

**Brand constraints to preserve:** the owner-approved hand-drawn stick-figure impression, expressive face, simple proportions, recognisable accessories and street-cartoon tone are design inputs—not a validated audience preference or proof of rights ownership. Review original artwork, fonts, animations, sound and voice commercial rights before publishing. Avoid a production-heavy animated show in the first month.

### Six low-cost pilot formats

| ID | Format / concrete premise | Tests | Primary outcome | Misleading metric / failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | **“Doddy forgot his own basketball.”** Silent visual gag; recognisable face/backpack | Memorable character and repeat-watch curiosity | Eligible profile follows, return viewers where platform reports consistently | Laughs do not imply app interest |
| **C2** | **“Doddy catches your ‘I’ll do it tomorrow’ excuse.”** Comedy about forgotten follow-through, no product claim | Pain recognition independent of app demo | Eligible link visits / actual last-episode descriptions | Relatability with no unresolved pain |
| **C3** | **Same follow-through task, Doddy presentation.** One verified demo with exact limitations | Character + utility comprehension | Qualified task-page action / eventual completed task | Edited demo pretending unsupported feature works |
| **C4** | **Same script and task, neutral UI presentation.** Match duration, CTA, quality and reach allocation with C3 | Incremental character effect | Difference in **qualified** conversion with intervals | Different pacing/algorithmic audiences confound result |
| **C5** | **“Does Doddy actually beat Siri?”** Honest side-by-side, including failure | Credibility and baseline relevance | Demo-to-test opt-in; uncoached task outcomes | Cherry-picked weak baseline or misleading claims |
| **C6** | **“Doddy tries being a translator.”** Comic scripted moment + explicitly labelled real/simulated demo | Translation interest as separate job | Qualified translation cohort signups and real frequency | Viewers want jokes, not translation app |

**[P] Initial production target:** roughly **6–12 short videos in 3–4 weeks**, favouring recycled character rigs, voice and simple edits. This is an execution capacity experiment, **not** sufficient by itself to establish causal creative performance. Log actual storyboard/animation/edit/caption hours. Cap production if it prevents interviews or prototype benchmarking.

### Causal vs observational creative testing

For initial organic discovery, compare **C1 entertainment** and **C2 pain** and **C3 demo** only as *directional channel-content fit*. **Do not claim “the character caused X% better conversion.”** For incremental character impact, compare **C3 vs C4** with equivalent job, script, CTA, language, exposure and placements; allocate comparable visitors where practical (ideally randomised landing page or paid matched cells with capped spend). In a factorial design, manipulate character appearance (yes/no) and task clarity (demonstration/story) separately. With small cells, expect **INCONCLUSIVE**, not a fabricated winner.

### Attribution: how social content reaches the product

`video_id + channel → unique first-party landing visit (where lawful) → adult/iPhone eligibility → explicit beta interest → permitted install/TestFlight → first verified target-job success → natural repeat D7/D14/D30 → paid action (later)`.

Keep a visible **unattributed/unknown** category, tag creative IDs without covert cross-site profiling, preserve consistent counting windows. Do not use App Store Connect campaign links as the sole prelaunch solution (small-cohort reporting and availability limits). [EX15]

**Character-only pivot:** if people rewatch and share but do not qualify for or complete repeat assistant jobs, treat Doddy as a possible independent IP/media experiment. Reassess entertainment monetisation separately; **do not** book hypothetical character-related app revenue.

### Interview prompts that reduce leading

Ask **before showing Doddy**: “Tell me the last time you had to remember or follow through on something while using your phone.” “When was it?” “What did you do first?” “Show me how you would usually handle it.” “What happened when that method failed?” “When did you *not* use AI, and why?” “What would stop you using an additional app?” Capture neutral direct participant wording only when genuinely recorded and permissioned; **none exists yet**.

### Comparison protocol

- Let the participant select **their best already available** tool and paid plan; verify installed versions and entitlements.
- Counterbalance test order, avoid training only on Doddy, and label artificial tasks.
- Start timer at *natural trigger*, including launch and permission/configuration, not at response-stream start.
- Score objective completion and harmful error independently of enjoyment.
- Collect user effort/trust as secondary outcomes; capture environment (public/noisy/private), language and network.
- Record refusals and setup abandonment, not just completers.
- Preserve a participant-level confidentiality boundary; no raw transcript needed in aggregate metrics by default.

### Minimal instrumentation & formulas

**Events** (privacy-minimised): `source_view` where lawful; `eligible_landing`; `beta_invite_accept`; `first_open`; `job_trigger_observed`; `job_started`; `permission_prompted`; `permission_granted/declined`; `job_completed_verified`; `job_failed`; `user_correction`; `natural_repeat`; `baseline_reversion`; `content_hours`; `model_cost_event`; `purchase/refund`. Each event needs definition, timestamp/cohort, anonymous research ID where necessary, lawful purpose, retention and data owner. Do **not** attach private prompts, precise location or voice to analytics events unless essential and separately justified.

- `qualified_activation_rate = users with first verified target-job success / eligible beta installs` (also report from eligible landing denominator).
- `natural_reuse_D14 = users completing ≥1 unprompted real target job during days 8–14 / cohort users eligible and observed in that window` **and** report the standard all-cohort denominator. Record whether an actual trigger occurred.
- `opportunity_adjusted_repeat = users choosing Doddy at a later natural trigger / observed users with at least one later eligible trigger`, alongside absolute return and censoring.
- `failure_rate = attempts with verified failure or unacceptable error / attempted jobs`.
- `cost_per_activated = attributable channel + production cost / qualified first-job completers`; show organic cash-only and fully burdened cost separately.
- `cost_per_repeater = attributable channel + production cost / qualified natural repeaters`.
- `per_payer_contribution = realised net receipts after taxes/Store commissions/refunds − actual AI/STT/TTS/tool usage − variable serving/support` (allocate shared fixed cost separately; disclose formula assumptions).
- `payback_period = acquisition cost / positive measured period contribution` **only when enough payment and renewal history exists**. Until then display scenarios, not estimated LTV as a fact.

**No synthetic dashboards:** if zero real users, every Doddy-specific metric must be `NOT MEASURED`; never populate with market benchmarks.

### Unit-economics worksheet—formula, not a forecast

For each **actual** product offer, model per customer:

`net_receipts = charged_price - VAT/taxes (as applicable) - App Store/platform fee - refunds/chargebacks`

`variable_service_cost = voice_in_minutes×observed_STT_rate + voice_out_minutes×observed_TTS_rate + input/output_model_usage×actual_provider_rate + tool/API_requests×actual_rate + variable_storage/network + variable_support`

`variable_contribution = net_receipts - variable_service_cost`

`gross_payback_budget = variable_contribution_per_period × conservative number_of_paid_periods_observed_or_scenario`

`CAC_ceiling = less_than_or_equal_to defensible conservative payback budget`, subject to an explicit cash runway and risk allowance.

**Never mix** RevenueCat's median revenue-per-payer, conversion and subscriber-retention figures into a synthetic expected LTV. Show P50 / P90 / high-user cost percentiles only once real usage observations exist; analyse even a single extreme-cost beta user as a risk. Advertising CAC is unaffordable if positive gross contribution cannot credibly cover acquisition.

## 10. Decision gates: proceed, repeat, pivot or stop

**These are [P] governance thresholds.** Decide numerical effect sizes and observations **before** seeing outcomes; qualify unknowns as **INCONCLUSIVE** rather than “passed.” Research-stage thresholds are not PMF proof.

| Decision | **Minimum evidence to GO (provisional)** | HOLD / REVISE / STOP |
|---|---|---|
| **D1. Continue character development** | Reproducible creative production without derailing research; some *eligible* adult interest and repeat exposure or separate media rationale; clear legal/IP chain | HOLD scaling if labour is excessive. Pivot to media-only if enjoyment exists but no software transfer. Stop unsafe/deceptive content. |
| **D2. Continue investing in app** | One cohort + natural trigger + unmet consequence + plausible testable permitted action, not just praise | If all incumbent paths are adequate or episodes rare, stop current wedge. |
| **D3. Select initial ICP** | Distinct cohort with ≥8–12 specific episodes across ≥5 independent people, including ≥3 prospective records; document negatives and broad-screen denominator | Do not promote a convenience sample to a Poland prevalence estimate. |
| **D4. Commit to primary job** | D3 + observed incumbent friction + target outcome/quality and permissions; ideally a small comparator showing potential | If comparator fails, change one job hypothesis, not add all features. |
| **D5. Rebuild technical foundation** | D4 + current prototype audit + scoped requirements + material limitation of prototype; documented ownership, security and data boundaries | New repo only with a **one-job milestone**, not a speculative full agent framework. |
| **D6. Start controlled beta** | Reproducible end-to-end task, no unresolved severe data/safety/security issue, adult selection/privacy notice, incident rollback, necessary Apple TestFlight review | Do not send public builds with unknown data flows or misrepresented actions. |
| **D7. Test monetisation** | Observed natural-use value, defined premium benefit and usage limits, conservative service-cost trace, properly described paid offer | Positive intentions or waiting list alone fail the gate. |
| **D8. Increase channel spend** | Measured eligible source→verified task→natural repeat path; predeclared cost ceiling/payback horizon, conservative positive contribution and compliance gate | If attribution weak or repeat usage absent, only small capped learning spend; never declare affordable CAC from views. |

### More precise evidence progression

- **Gate 0 — Legal/measurement admissibility:** research protocol, asset rights and minimal data flow accepted by responsible humans for the *specific* activity. A material unresolved security/legal risk is a **hard stop for that activity**.
- **Gate 1 — Problem existence (D01 provisional):** real independent episodes and missed outcomes; hypothetical plans insufficient. Proposed episode counts above come from D01 Evaluation §6.2 as *management minima*.
- **Gate 2 — Task advantage:** preregister practical effect (e.g. +15pp verified task success **or** ≥20% less end-to-end elapsed time) and **noninferiority on severe errors**; these are illustrative candidate thresholds from D01 Evaluation, **not externally calibrated**. Include confidence/intervals and the participant's best substitute.
- **Gate 3 — Repeat usefulness:** next naturally occurring trigger and uncoached choice of Doddy, observed for the prespecified job and cohort, with zero-trigger weeks and dropouts. No arbitrary “30% D30” without job frequency and sample context.
- **Gate 4 — Distribution evidence:** qualified source→task→repeat, with accurate denominators, missing attribution, real production labour, sensible capped exposure. Do not confuse brand popularity with retention.
- **Gate 5 — Commercial readiness:** real costs, actual paid behaviour/renewals where obtainable, bounded high-usage downside and required compliance. If evidence has not matured, **HOLD**, not GO.

**Founder verdict categories:** `GO_NARROW`; `CONTINUE_EXPERIMENT`; `PIVOT_TO_DIFFERENT_JOB`; `PIVOT_CHARACTER_ONLY`; `STOP`; `INCONCLUSIVE_NEED_MORE_DATA`. Maintain versions and reason for each decision.

### Conditions that could invalidate the strategy outright

- Repeated independent observations show no consequential unmet everyday task among eligible users.
- Native Reminders/Translate or available ChatGPT workflows consistently outperform the real Doddy journey including launch, permissions and errors.
- Character viewers are largely ineligible or refuse to activate/reuse the app even when offered a real job.
- A character increases time-in-app only through rambling, induced dependence or pressure, not user benefit.
- The voice/memory safety and legal constraints cannot be resolved at sustainable cost.
- High usage plus refunds or short paid tenure make conservative contribution negative; no defensible acquisition payback exists.

## 12. What NOT to do (even if the demo looks impressive)

1. **Do not immediately build “Siri + ChatGPT + Character.AI + all phone tools.”** It creates switching, legal and operating costs before a known job.
2. **Do not commit to a permanent unrestricted iOS overlay, background assistant or default double-tap assignment.** Treat real Apple documented launch methods as constraints until demonstrated otherwise.
3. **Do not build a vector DB, knowledge graph, multimodal lifetime-memory engine or full cloud sync merely because the product is described as “personal.”** Start with consented explicit facts only if a test requires them; local-first where practical.
4. **Do not confuse users with conversations, fans with customers, or app opens with successfully completed tasks.**
5. **Do not build paid acquisition forecasts using modeled website visits, social ad reach, RevenueCat medians or Google Trends 0–100 charts.**
6. **Do not run endless content production at the expense of seeing real user episodes.** Create a reusable animation rig and track hours.
7. **Do not imply every Doddy video is a live app demo.** Mark story, simulation and actual product footage honestly.
8. **Do not interpret a 2-week novelty bump as subscription retention or product-market fit.**
9. **Do not assume all private chats/audio can or should be retained.** Define minimisation, deletion, provider handling and explicit context controls.
10. **Do not overwrite the original GitHub repository while designing V2.** Audit read-only; if justified, establish a separate codebase and clear milestone.

## 13. Executive answers and founder priorities

### Direct answers to the ten required strategic questions

1. **Does Doddy merit more effort?** **Yes, bounded**, because category familiarity and a distinctive creative direction allow cheap falsifiable tests. No commercial viability has been shown.

10. **Five immediate actions?** Create the decision register; recruit an episode-based cohort; audit prototype; audit the competing workflow on a real phone; produce a small matched character/pain/demo content batch.

### If I were building Doddy myself

I would **not** treat the character as optional garnish, nor pretend it replaces the need for utility. I'd spend the first two weeks interviewing people with very recent examples of “I meant to do that, but I lost it,” watching them use their current iPhone tools, and checking whether the prototype can reliably save or retrieve one intention. If the user cohort and unit economics stay weak after that, I'd choose character-only or stop rather than broaden the app to hide failure.
