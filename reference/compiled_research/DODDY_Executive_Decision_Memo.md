# DODDY | Executive investment decision
**8 October 2026 · Recommendation: VALIDATE FIRST**

## Decision
Do **not** fund a broad iOS autonomous assistant or a long character-only prelaunch campaign. Run **90 days of parallel character content plus a functional narrow prototype**, with a suggested incremental experimental cash cap of **$5,000–$15,000** excluding founder time and sunk prototype work.

## Why
Pew's 2026 US sample found 49% of adults had ever used chatbots and 24% used them daily; regular conversation with AI is established. Yet the most common stated applications were information search (42% of all adults) and work among employed adults (38%), against only 4% of adults for companionship. RevenueCat's 2026 AI subscription cohorts show just 6.1% of AI monthly subscribers retained after 12 months. Character-first marketing has examples, but there is no credible public evidence that a *new fictional character's* followers will turn into long-lived paying productivity app customers. Sources: https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/ ; https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps

## Product wedge
**"Doddy remembers the little things so you can get on with life."** Adults 18–34, initial use case capture → retrieve → follow-through. Keep voice optional; no open-ended dependence-first product. Test character-rich UX against an equally functional no-character control.

## What a first version needs
Reliable editable memory, correct later recall, reminders with user approval, fast text response, optional limited voice, clear permissions, deletion/export, adult safety posture, event logging on task outcomes. An expressive cartoon and animated onboarding support, but must not interfere.

## Most important commercial constraint
At an illustrative **$9.99/month** including modeled VAT and 15% App Store fee, net receipts are roughly **$7.08**. At the current listed **$0.05/min GPT-Live voice rate**, five minutes daily costs **$7.50/month before other inference or infrastructure**, destroying per-user contribution. A usage cap or alternative low-cost speech stack is indispensable. https://platform.openai.com/pricing ; https://developer.apple.com/support/payment-options-on-the-app-store-in-the-eu

## Go/no-go
Proceed to a limited paid beta **only if** non-friend users repeatedly complete a valued task, pay or enter genuine paid checkout, and serve costs stay positive. Treat exposure/view count as top-of-funnel only. A popular character and unsuccessful app is a **PIVOT** to character/media IP; no recurring task demand after two focused iterations is **STOP**. Before wider EU release complete documented Article 50 disclosure, GDPR assessment and child-protection review. https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august
