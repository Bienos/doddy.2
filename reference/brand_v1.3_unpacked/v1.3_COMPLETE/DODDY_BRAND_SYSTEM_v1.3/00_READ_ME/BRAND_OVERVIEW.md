# Brand premise

**Name:** Doddy

**Line:** Your mate in your pocket.

**Belief:** A capable AI mate should feel approachable, useful and unmistakably human in expression—not omniscient or intrusive.

**Visual codes:** round white/ivory head, tall oval eyes, slightly off-center smile, three expressive tufts, flexible black-ink body, mitten hands, oversized orange trainers, orange backpack, basketball, orange motion marks, warm paper and ink-dark setting.

**Voice:** friendly; capable; playful when context allows; calm and careful when it matters.

**Deployment:** character-first social storytelling; iPhone companion identity; a design system spanning static, motion, voice and product interaction.
