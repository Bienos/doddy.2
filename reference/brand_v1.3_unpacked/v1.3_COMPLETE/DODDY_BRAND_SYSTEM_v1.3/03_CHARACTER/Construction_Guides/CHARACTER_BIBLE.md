# Doddy Character Design Bible v1.3

## Canonical silhouette

Character canvas: 420 × 580 units; head top around y=77 and base around y=255; centre x≈199; head ~185 units across; torso from y=255 to y=398; shoe baseline ~530. Ratio: approximately 2.9 head diameters tall (stylized digital asset; the recovered collage labels a *different* 5.5-head editorial view).

Required: organic nearly-round light face; **two black vertical oval pupils**; a slight asymmetrical curved smile; three simple hair tufts; flexible black one-stroke stick torso and limbs; mitten hands; high-volume trainers with orange upper/white sole; orange backpack; optional orange basketball and line accents.

## Stroke

Canonical ink stroke: 8–14 SVG units in 420x580 viewBox; rounded caps/joins. Mouth 8; eye shapes filled; character/head outline 9–10; limbs 11–14. Minor hand-made wobble is permitted; eyes and silhouette should not be replaced.

## Expressions

30 face sheets with names in `Expressions/`. Preserve pupil geometry and facial framing. Expression intensity 0–3: 0 neutral, 1 conversational, 2 enthusiastic, 3 celebration only. Sensitive topics use 0–1.

## Do

Use clear poses; small asymmetry; lively accessory accents; maintain legible eyes at 32px; use textured paper around rather than directly behind essential UI labels.

## Don't

Do not turn him into a shiny 3D avatar, robot, animal or corporate jelly mascot. Do not add detailed fingers, hair, clothing or pupils without a design review. Do not reshape the head/eyes between contexts. Do not imply Doddy has heard, remembered or acted without actual permission/confirmation.

## Small-size tiers

≥128px: full character + accessories permitted. 64–127px: head and orange mark only. 32–63px: head, pupils, smile only. <32px: use a simplified head glyph tested at native scale.

## Art approval warning

These fresh vectors approximate the recovered approved style; illustrator should reconcile line character, footwear detail and 3/4 turnaround before the canonical master is frozen.
