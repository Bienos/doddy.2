# DODDY CHARACTER BIBLE v1.3

## Canonical drawing geometry (source artboard 500 x 600 units)

- Face center (246,149), radius 79 units; slightly imperfect circle is acceptable, the recognisable proportions are not negotiable.
- Eyes: two vertical ovals, typically x219 and x272, y152. Narrow black silhouettes: ~7x18 units. Eye spacing ~53 units.
- Smile: upward open asymmetric ink curve from (214,192) via (248,232) to (284,190); shift control point slightly by mood.
- Top head hair/sparks: three black swept dashes; angle varies but quantity is kept 2-3.
- Body: one black central pen stroke, neck (245,232) to hip (247,352); ~13-unit stroke, 10-13 for arms/legs.
- Hands: simplified dark mitten silhouette. No five-finger realism.
- Shoes: oversized white trainers with orange panels and black ink soles; feet visibly larger than hands.
- Backpack: orange rounded daypack placed behind the right shoulder.
- Basketball: orange, line-drawn; accessory, not a permanent anatomical feature.

## Face invariant

Keep the large circular head, 2 upright oval eyes, smiling/expressive asymmetric mouth, and hand-drawn black ink. Simplify detail as the character gets smaller; never scale a full-body figure directly to 24px.

## Do

- Use broad, imperfect ink strokes and slight organic variation.
- Respect recognisable head-to-body ratio (~1:2.5 head diameter to figure height).
- Keep backpack and shoes visually related across poses.
- Use orange motion marks as punctuation, not permanent rays.
- Keep full-body character ≥80px tall for recognisability; face-only under this threshold.

## Don't

- Replace with a robot/animal/3D emoji.
- Add pupils, glossy 3D eyes, realistic limbs, or detailed fingernails.
- Use thick outlines inconsistently.
- Animate limbs discontinuously or teleport between states.
- Claim character variants as validated animation keyframes; they are vector illustration poses.
